# Chakra and Gann Source Audit

Date: 2026-07-15 IST

Scope: Sarvatobhadra Chakra, Sudarshana Chakra, W. D. Gann's *The Tunnel Thru the Air*, and public financial-astrology forum discussions. This audit controls local-RAG ingestion and evidence-only app planning. It does not certify any market signal.

## Executive decision

- Sarvatobhadra can enter the app only as a separate research lab after its exact grid convention is selected and certified. It is not ready for Auto Suggest.
- Sudarshana can enter the app only as a separate three-reference timing explorer after a specific BPHS recension and translation are selected. It is not ready for Auto Suggest.
- Gann's 1927 novel is lawful to index from Project Gutenberg as literature and a hypothesis source. No hidden trading code is assumed.
- Forex Factory material is retained only as pre-registration ideas. Forum claims are not doctrine, evidence, or permission to edit a rule.

## Sarvatobhadra findings

The local 1937 Subrahmanya Sastri *Phaladeepika* scan contains two evidence layers that were previously labeled too broadly:

1. PDF page 341, chapter 26 verse 48, is Mantreswara's instruction to consider auspicious and inauspicious Sarvatobhadra vedha when judging transit.
2. PDF pages 342-356 are an editor-added supplement. The edition says the slokas are extracted from other works and later identifies the English rendering as material from *Horaratna*. Those pages must not be cited as though every rule were Mantreswara's own verse.

The source registry now distinguishes those layers. The editor supplement describes an 81-cell form, 28 nakshatras including Abhijit, letters, signs, tithi classes, and vedha rules. A Government Oriental Manuscripts Library catalogue separately records a `sarvatobhadracakraslokah` witness and describes both 64-cell and 81-cell forms. A second manuscript catalogue records a complete thirteen-page Sanskrit work attributed to Narhari. These records establish textual plurality rather than one uncontested formula.

Sources:

- [1937 Phaladeepika scan](https://archive.org/details/in.ernet.dli.2015.92117)
- [Sanskrit Documents Phaladeepika transcription](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_sociology_astrology/phaladIpika.html)
- [Government Oriental Manuscripts Library entry 14002](https://www.wisdomlib.org/history/compilation/catalogue-sanskrit-manuscripts-madras/d/doc1538063.html)
- [Narhari manuscript catalogue record](https://manuscripts.vitragelibrary.org/collection-details/sarvatobhadra-chakra)

### App candidate

A dedicated `Chakra Lab` surface may later provide a 9 by 9 grid, current transit timestamp, selected source convention, vedha trace, and exportable evidence. Every cell must disclose why it contains a nakshatra, letter, sign, or tithi class. The first version should not output bullish, bearish, entry, exit, or confidence labels.

Required before implementation:

- translate or externally review every placement rule used;
- define Abhijit boundaries, tithi grouping, direct/retrograde vedha, nodes, benefic/malefic state, and ayanamsa;
- build source-page fixtures;
- validate market usefulness only after the doctrine engine is frozen.

## Sudarshana findings

The local page-structured 1899 BPHS Purva-Uttara witness does not expose a Sudarshana chapter under that name. Later BPHS editions do, but numbering differs: one circulating English edition labels it chapter 74, while a 2006 edition catalogue labels it chapter 76. This is a recension warning, not a minor formatting difference.

The common later-edition description uses three concentric twelve-house references from Lagna, Moon, and Sun and applies year/month/short-period progression. The user-supplied Sanjay Rath book is useful secondary commentary, but its later caveat that some illustrated methods are not foolproof prevents it from certifying an engine.

Sources:

- [Sanskrit Documents BPHS index and scan links](https://sanskritdocuments.org/iast/jyotisha/)
- [Later English edition with chapter 74](https://vedic-astro.s3.amazonaws.com/books/bhrihat_parasara_hora_shastra.pdf)
- [2006 edition catalogue with chapter 76](https://www.wisdomlib.org/shop/books/jyotisha/brihat-parashara-hora-shastra/doc234250.html)

### App candidate

A dedicated `Sudarshana` pane may later render three aligned twelve-house rings for Lagna, Moon, and Sun, an age-period navigator, and a source-backed decision trail. It should remain outside the candlestick chart. It needs deterministic natal computations from the existing Raman/Swiss Ephemeris contract, not calculations generated by the LLM.

Required before implementation:

- select a BPHS recension and a legally usable translation;
- reconcile chapter/verse identity against at least one independent witness;
- define annual, monthly, and shorter-period progression exactly;
- produce reference-chart fixtures and obtain external adjudication;
- keep all conclusions descriptive until walk-forward market testing.

## Gann text findings

*The Tunnel Thru the Air* was first published in 1927. Project Gutenberg provides a proofread transcription and states that it is public domain in the United States. The original scan is also catalogued through Internet Archive/Open Library. The novel's foreword says it contains a secret in veiled language; that is evidence of authorial intent, not evidence for any specific planetary code.

Sources:

- [Project Gutenberg edition 74988](https://www.gutenberg.org/ebooks/74988)
- [Original 1927 scan](https://archive.org/details/tunnelthruairorl00gann)
- [Open Library bibliographic record](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL25587496M/The_tunnel_thru_the_air)

The local RAG now indexes the Project Gutenberg text as `GANN_TUNNEL_1927` with authority `public_domain_hypothesis_source_not_doctrine`.

### Responsible decoding sequence

1. Extract all explicit dates, times, numbers, names, Bible citations, market actions, and chapter locations without looking at market outcomes.
2. Publish each proposed decoding transform before testing it.
3. Test against untouched periods, shuffled dates, and at least one comparison novel of similar length and era.
4. Correct for the number of transforms attempted.
5. Preserve failed hypotheses. A transform that changes after every miss is not a forecast method.

## Forex Factory findings

The most relevant public discussions found were `W. D. Gann & His Fellows`, `W.D. Gann planetary price & timing analysis`, and `Planetary Cycles Trading (EUR/USD)`. They discuss instrument radix dates, transit/progression to radix, seasonality, lunations/eclipses, planetary price lines, North Node scaling, selected planetary pairs, and multi-factor confluence. The strongest methodological statement is also the least glamorous: log every repeat and backtest it. Claims of guaranteed outcomes, undisclosed planets/degrees, and retrospectively selected anchors are unusable as evidence.

Sources:

- [W. D. Gann & His Fellows](https://www.forexfactory.com/thread/1182419-w-d-gann-his-fellows)
- [W.D. Gann planetary price & timing analysis](https://www.forexfactory.com/thread/945113-wd-gann-planetary-price-timing-analysis)

The curated local source `FINANCIAL_ASTRO_FORUM_HYPOTHESES` converts the useful claims into fixed test candidates and records the required negative controls. It is excluded from direct inference by policy.

## Promotion gates

1. Provenance: lawful hashed edition, page/verse identity, content-layer labels.
2. Determinism: every formula and boundary encoded outside the LLM with fixtures.
3. Doctrine parity: independent implementation or qualified human adjudication.
4. Market evidence: frozen feature contract, purged walk-forward testing, baselines, multiple-testing correction, and retained failures.

Until all four pass, Chakra and Gann-derived fields are retrieval context or experimental features only. MT5 remains read-only and execution remains disabled.

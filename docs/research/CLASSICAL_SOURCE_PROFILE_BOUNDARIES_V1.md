# Classical Source Profile Boundaries V1

## Non-negotiable boundary

One calculation names one source profile. The application must not silently
blend a root text, editor supplement, commentary, reprint, calculator setting,
or fitted market rule into a new synthetic doctrine.

| Profile | May provide | Must not provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `SBC_PHALADEEPIKA_EDITOR_V1` | Page-certified selected Vedha directions, nature and isolated modifiers | Mantreswara root-text attribution, unresolved stacks, complete SBC grid or financial conversion |
| `SBC_TRAILOKYA_1972_V1` | Partial Trailokya commentary evidence and explicit unresolved states | Absolute orientation, complete grid, disputed Arghya values or market-price formula without a packet |
| `BPHS_1899_V1` | Candidate classical Shadbala, Drik and lordship rules after page certification | Silent replacement with JHora/PyJHora behavior |
| `PHALADEEPIKA_1937_V1` | Candidate root-text strength, nature, aspect and Ashtakavarga rules | Editor-supplement rules unless that distinct profile is named |
| `BRIHAT_JATAKA_1905_V1` | Independent classical cross-check | A combined formula averaged with BPHS or Phaladeepika |
| `SURYA_SIDDHANTA_1858_V1` | Time/motion/declination definitions and test fixtures | Replacement of Swiss Ephemeris operational positions without a separate astronomy decision |
| `AGARWAL_FINANCIAL_V1` | Modern practitioner commentary, clearly labelled | Classical or execution authority; complete formula claims from incomplete scans |
| `KAS_RESEARCH_V1` | Isolated modern research ablations | Mode 1 promotion or classical authority |
| `ENGINEERING_DISPLAY_V1` | Normalization, range display and explicit pair arithmetic | Classical score, doctrine or source-derived market polarity |

## Required separation

1. **Classical raw contribution** carries its source profile, locator, target,
   nature, motion, dignity, multiplier, unresolved reason and hash.
2. **Engineering display transform** may normalize or compare available raw
   inputs but never adds an astrological value or hides unknown coverage.
3. **Financial interpretation** is a separate modern contract. USD-supportive
   minus JPY-supportive is not classical currency doctrine.
4. **Software witnesses** are comparator metadata. JHora, PyJHora and a
   same-lineage reprint cannot be recorded as a primary classical source.

When profiles disagree, render `CONFLICTED` or allow a founder to select one
named profile. Never calculate an average doctrine.

# Trailokya Argha Modern Commodity Validation Plan V1

This is a future research design. It does not authorize data collection, fitting, source completion, or market use.

## Research question

Does a frozen, source-derived Trailokya Argha state contain incremental out-of-sample information about subsequent commodity availability/scarcity or price behavior beyond simple market and calendar baselines?

## Preconditions

1. Complete the missing source operator dependencies and preserve all literal anomalies.
2. Freeze a historical commodity ontology before looking at outcomes. Each mapping is `EXACT_MODERN_MATCH`, `LIKELY_MODERN_MATCH`, `BROAD_CATEGORY_ONLY`, `AMBIGUOUS`, or `NO_MAPPING`; only defensible exact mappings may enter the first study.
3. Begin in commodity domains, not FX: metals, grains, energy, or agricultural commodities only after data and mapping approval.
4. Make source state, commodity mapping, horizons and test plan append-only before evaluation.

## Comparator models

- `M0`: naive/calendar baseline.
- `M1`: market-only trend and volatility baseline.
- `M2`: simple astronomical-event baseline.
- `M3`: frozen source Argha state.
- `M4`: market baseline plus frozen Argha state.

Value requires out-of-sample incremental evidence over the relevant simpler baseline; an attractive isolated backtest is not evidence.

## Evaluation safeguards

Use purged walk-forward splits, horizon embargoes, untouched holdout periods, multiple-testing correction, ablations, placebo/time-shift controls, event randomization and a prospective append-only period. Log all hypotheses, parameter combinations, mappings, horizons and rejected models. Do not post-hoc invert signs, remap commodities or change a source table after seeing outcomes.

## Future state vector design

Keep source stages separate: scope, candidate ruler provenance, strength-component availability, Vedha/aspect gate state, relationship table result, five-category Viswa, benefic/malefic totals, residual, commodity basis, ontology state and unknown reasons. It must never collapse unknown into neutral or blend source Argha with SBC, price or an execution decision.

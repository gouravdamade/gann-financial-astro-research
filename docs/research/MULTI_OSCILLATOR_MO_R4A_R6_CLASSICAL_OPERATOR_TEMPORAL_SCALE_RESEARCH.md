## Temporal Operator Ledger

### What the source does establish

Trailokya's verses 65-79 (scan pp. 32-36; printed pp. 16-20) establish
source-named motion categories and descriptive residence material. Verse 58
(scan p. 30; printed p. 14) and verse 79 (scan p. 36; printed p. 20) also
delegate exact position/state determination to appropriate astronomical
calculation for the place and time. These are useful provenance and categorical
facts.

They do **not** establish an effect curve. In particular, they do not define a
universal moment at which an aspect or Vedha begins to have an effect, an exact
moment at which it stops, an application-versus-separation weighting, a period
of persistence, a decay profile, or a nested-wave hierarchy.

### `D_i` is an engineering hypothesis, not a strength claim

For an event whose independent geometry contract supplies both endpoints,

```text
D_i = separatingEndUtc - applyingStartUtc
```

is deterministically computable. R6 classifies any use of this quantity as
`ENGINEERING_MAPPING`: a future experiment may use it as an explicitly labelled
temporal-scale covariate or ablation partition. It cannot be labelled a
classical magnitude, strength, or effect duration. It cannot promote a rule to
Mode 1, create a smooth curve, infer persistence, or supply market direction.

The current atomic half-open interval is therefore event geometry only:

```text
[applyingStartUtc, separatingEndUtc)
```

It is not a source-defined efficacy window.

### Waves within waves

The source material supports neither a causal hierarchy between nested events
nor a rule that repeated, simultaneous, or parent/child events amplify one
another. Motion residence descriptions do not fill that gap. No repeated-hit
count, multi-scale aggregation, smoothing, or inherited duration is permitted
from this record.

## Magnitude, Gates, and Modifiers

Trailokya's Sthana Bala and Sthana Phala records are distinct source
measurements. Verse 162 expressly keeps Bala and Phala distinct; verses 163-165
record bounded Sthana Phala values in their stated context. Verse 166 records
isolated modifiers. These facts may be displayed or independently reproduced
with their locators.

They do not close any of the following:

- a composite strength total;
- a universal order for dignity, relation, motion, and modifier interaction;
- a universal multi-planet precedence or cancellation rule;
- a market magnitude, volatility, or monetary-size interpretation.

Accordingly, no value is summed, normalized, multiplied across conditions, or
translated to a financial magnitude in R6. A named context rule remains usable
only within its named context. It may not be generalized into a generic resolver.

### Recommended Experiment

R6 recommends exactly one next experiment:

`R6_CANDIDATE_C_SOURCE_PROFILED_UNSIGNED_REPRODUCTION_AND_ABSTENTION`

Two independently built evaluators would process a fresh, preregistered event
population and must reproduce the source-closed categories exactly while also
returning `UNKNOWN` for every declared unclosed condition. It uses no market
data, no price outcome, no return, and no currency polarity. That makes it
independent of S4, falsifiable, and useful for deciding whether a later,
separately governed market-bridge proposal is even technically and
provenance-wise coherent.

Evaluator A and Evaluator B must be separate implementations. They may share
only the frozen input population, exact astronomy inputs, immutable source
packet/contract identities, schemas, and declared semantic rules. They may not
import one another's business logic or source-category helpers, or use expected
outputs generated by either implementation. Each implementation identity and
source hash must be frozen before execution and before either output is
inspected. A mismatch fails that frozen run; neither evaluator may be patched
afterward to continue the same run. Source adjudication or implementation
correction requires a new preregistered run.

This agreement tests deterministic implementation reproducibility of the
already source-certified contracts. It does not independently prove that the
underlying classical interpretation is historically true beyond the accepted
source packets, and it does not validate market prediction.

This is deliberately an empirical reproducibility experiment, not a new
predictive market pilot. Passing it does not authorize a market-outcome test,
much less production use.

# Financial houses, yogas and qualifications

For Chart 44 the Leo ascendant gives the following whole-sign ownerships. House 1 Sun, 2 Mercury, 3 Venus, 4 Mars, 5 Jupiter, 6 Saturn, 7 Saturn, 8 Jupiter, 9 Mars, 10 Venus, 11 Mercury, 12 Moon. Count a house inclusively as 1 + ((sign − ascendant) mod 12). Change the origin to the Moon only where the text specifies it. Thus Cancer can be both the twelfth natal house and the second lunar house. The two facts describe Saturn's placement from two origins.

**Strength has several jobs.** In XLI.9 the Sun's own sign is a necessary input. In XLI.17 strength qualifies the reading after the combination is identified. In Raman's Chart 40 his treatment of an exalted Jupiter's aspect modifies a debilitated Moon's second-house indication. That is a worked exception in a different chart. In Chart 44 Raman uses the second lord's friendly sign and its navāṃśa placement qualitatively. Mercury's divisional Lagna position verifies on the 29 September calculation despite two other D9 discrepancies. No numerical Śaḍbala threshold, Iṣṭa/Kaṣṭa ratio, avasthā multiplier or retrogression rule appears in the Chart 44 judgment. Their computational existence supplies no operand here.

**Supporting comparison.** Sārāvalī XXXIII.82 ties multiple sources of gain to capable occupants in the ascendant, second and eleventh. Its house test fails in Chart 44 because the rāśi ascendant contains no planet. An unquantified strength adjective would still need a source-specific interpretation in a chart with all three houses occupied.

**Acquisition boundary.** The search covered Raman's published worked nativities, financial passages and pre-event documentation leads. Charts 40, 41 and 44 met the author-worked criterion. Chart 44 supplied the clearest debt reasoning with an explicit parent and child. The earlier Cancer-lagna livelihood example in Aiyar X.1 remains useful as an authorial hypothetical. None of these establishes an independently dated prior prediction of the Chart 44 debt. No comparison with market prices or investigation of subsequent market outcomes was necessary.

**Project baseline.** Read the eight specified AIW-MASTER-03 reports on financial framework, daśā and yoga, categorical predicates, hybrid architecture, gaps, roadmap, model comparison and double counting. Their unanswered financial-period case is the starting point. The new evidence is R44, its two date trials and both balance replays, the distinction between commentary profiles in VIII.23, and the limited local operators catalogued here.

# A debt crisis in Raman’s Chart 44

**Evidence level B, retrospective worked nativity.** B. V. Raman gives the birth data, two chart diagrams, a period balance, an account of loss and the reasons he assigns to it. He reports loss of fortune and debt of Rs. 50,000 in Ketu mahādaśā, Venus bhukti. That amount belongs to his narrative. The procedure below can recover the indicated kind of harm and the period he names from his account. It cannot generate the amount. No independently dated pre-event version was located. 1. **Identify the question.** This is a person's earnings, spending and debt. The second from Leo is Virgo, containing the Sun, the Lagna lord. Saturn in Cancer aspects Virgo by its third-place aspect. Raman judges the wealth house ordinary.
2. **Follow its owner.** Mercury rules Virgo and occupies Libra with Venus. Raman treats Venus's ownership of houses 3 and 10 as adverse here. Mercury occupies the divisional ascendant. The author rates the second lord ordinary despite the friendly sign.
3. **Retain the favorable indication.** Jupiter, lord of the fifth, occupies the fourth with Mars, ruler of the fourth and ninth. Raman gives this combination a favorable reading. An otherwise favorable earning capacity can coexist with a poor ability to retain money.
4. **Change the reference explicitly.** The second sign from the Gemini Moon is Cancer, occupied by Saturn and Rahu. Raman associates this with spending the earnings. He also identifies adjacent, second/twelfth sign relationships in the two diagrams as a reason for instability. His prose gives no weighted total or exhaustive list of pairs.
5. **Trace the debt claim.** Ketu occupies Capricorn, sixth from Leo. Its host and the sixth lord, Saturn, occupies Cancer, twelfth from Leo and second from the Moon. Raman attributes the crisis to Ketu’s period and specifically identifies Venus’s subperiod. Venus's natal association with Mercury supplies relevant context. The text does not say that this fact alone selects Venus over every other subperiod.
6. **Check timing independently.** The printed Jupiter balance and Vimśottarī sequence place Ketu–Venus at age 49y 6m 17d to 50y 8m 17d in the period notation. The financial conclusion is an attributed case judgment. These endpoints describe its eligible interval, not the duration of debt. ### Why Venus qualifies, and why it is not unique

Raman gives a selection procedure earlier in the same chapter. Construct the union of the second lord, aspectors of the second, its occupants, aspectors of its lord, companions of its lord and the lord of the second counted from the Moon. A planet entering by any route can signify second-house affairs in its major or subsidiary period. When both rulers qualify the subject is emphasized. When just the child qualifies its effect is limited. When just the parent qualifies it is feeble. This is a rule for topical relevance. Direction still depends on ownership, aspects and placement. [Raman, pp. 91–93, PDF pp. 100–102](https://elibraryofyoga.com/server/api/core/bitstreams/2271a6e2-f4ca-49bd-9706-89cec2d6f5f5/content#page=100)

On Chart 44, the six channels give Mercury, Saturn, Sun, no additional classical planet, Venus, and Moon respectively under full aspects from the seven visible grahas. This recovers Venus **before** reading the stated event. Ketu’s sixth-house debt role comes from the case discussion. The earlier generic list does not establish Ketu as a direct second-house participant under this geometry. Node-aspect or dispositor substitutions would require a separate rule. A generally favorable Venus period in the catalogue on p. 93 cannot settle this individual outcome.

**Source-worked control.** In Chart 38 Raman himself enumerates those six channels and gets Venus, none, Mercury and Sun, Jupiter, none, and Saturn. Hence both Saturn as parent and Venus as child qualify. He associates that period with appointment to office. The procedure selects a financially relevant interval without predicting a salary amount. From the chart’s Mars balance of 4y 2m 14d, adding Rahu 18 and Jupiter 16 puts Saturn's start at 38y 2m 14d. The Saturn, Mercury and Ketu children use 1,083 + 969 + 399 period parts. Venus then occupies [45y 0m 5d, 48y 2m 5d). The interval is independently derived, the appointment is author-reported, and this is a separate native from Chart 44.

The original chart and the named outcome make this a stronger case than a constructed chart. The date notation, exact opening balance, one-to-one selection of Venus and civil dating remain open. Chart 40 (pp. 97–99) independently documents a prosperity judgment with both rulers named, while Chart 41 (pp. 99–100) names a Mercury period for improvement. They are neighboring examples by the same author and provide no independent witness to Chart 44.

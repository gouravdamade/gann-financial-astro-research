# A condition for harm to a native's finances

**Santhanam, BPHS I, XLII.13, printed p. 415 (PDF p. 414).** The printed Sanskrit reads *koṇeśadṛṣṭihīnā ye trikeśaiḥ saṃyutā grahāḥ*. It describes planets joined with the lords of difficult houses and deprived of the aspect of a trinal lord. The second line places the wealth harm in the planets’ own periods. Santhanam's English explicitly enumerates 6, 8 and 12 and allows association with one of their lords. I checked the page image. Verse 14 follows directly, without a separate note for verse 13. The cancellation by Mercury at 16–18 belongs to a different combination. [Santhanam’s bilingual page](https://storage.yandexcloud.net/j108/library/hr6fkynz/Maharishi_Parashara_-_Brihat_Parasara_Hora_Sastra_%28Vol._1%29.pdf#page=414)

**Textual predicate.** A personal financial subject, an identified planet P in its own active period, at least one qualifying association to P, and no qualifying trinal lord aspect **to P**. Existence of a single protective aspect makes the last condition false, even when another aspect is unknown. A failed test issues no opposite judgment. Amount, insolvency, recurrence and event date are unspecified.

**Geometry used for the fixtures.** Use whole-sign natal houses, traditional visible-planet ownerships and same-sign co-occupation by distinct planets for *saṃyuta*. Use directed full graha aspects, counting the occupied sign as 1. All seven have place 7, Mars also 4 and 8, Jupiter 5 and 9, Saturn 3 and 10. Include trinal lords 1, 5 and 9 and deduplicate planet identities. An aspect from a planet to itself has no place in this list. The general full-aspect convention also occurs in Raman’s *Hindu Predictive Astrology* XIII. XLII.13 by itself does not define an orb, an aspect-strength cutoff, whether fractional aspects suffice, or the scope of *koṇa* in every recension. A bare exchange of signs or a dispositor link is insufficient to prove a conjunction. Retain the chosen profile on every geometrical result. The wording-level rule can accept adjudicated testimony from another documented profile.

**Check the definition of a trine.** Cases 2 and 3 deliberately test the ascendant-lord convention. To test the same logic independently of that choice, put the Moon (lord of 12) beside Mars in Scorpio and Jupiter (lord of 5) in Cancer. The joining is proved by the Moon and the protective fifth-place aspect by Jupiter. The expected result is CONDITION_ABSENT whether the trinal set is {5,9} or {1,5,9}. With Jupiter's sign withheld, the Moon still proves joining, but the expected result becomes UNKNOWN in both profiles. The JSON retains both tests.

**Counterexample from the actual financial narrative.** Ketu is in Capricorn alone. Saturn owns that sign and opposes it from Cancer. Ownership or an incoming aspect from Saturn is a different relation from conjunction. Under the declared profile the required joining is absent even if Ketu is admitted as a period ruler. Raman’s account of loss therefore cannot verify XLII.13. The Mars-period exercise above assesses a different period and asserts no historical loss. It also illustrates an unresolved overlap with BPHS XLI.16–17 because Mars is the ninth lord. The passages supply distinct qualifications and no ordering between them.

# Source ledger: what was actually found

## Fresh primary-text and translation checks

**1. Parashari tradition, BPHS, digital Sanskrit, chapters 72-74.** [Sanskrit text, chapters 71-80](https://sanskritdocuments.org/doc_z_misc_sociology_astrology/par7180.html). 72.1-4: samudaya is assigned per sign (pratibham); >30 and 25..30 have different grades. This is not Phaladipika's 28 rule. No seven-different-sign worked example identified. Digital recension, not a critical edition.

**2. A genuinely aggregate numerical candidate, NOT AV:** same text, rashmiphala 73.1-7,19-22. Fold the arc from debility to the half-circle and scale by the planet's own exaltation rays; correction tradition includes dignity and combustion exceptions. Judgment uses the sum of rays; v19 specifies the birth-time sky. E.g. halfway from debility yields half that planet's maximum before corrections. This warrants a **separate planetary ray total**, not a weight on marks or the lunar ch28 I/K formula. Needed: collate the numerical names in v1 and alternate corrections against a printed witness and reproduce seven corrected terms from one published horoscope. Natal rays are constant; recomputing a new sky every hour changes the subject.

**3. Actual conditional synthesis:** same digital root, Sudarsana 74.27-28, gives joint use of time-specific house judgment and AV: concurrence permits the full judgment; discord invokes predominance by strength (balakramat). This is stronger than merely juxtaposing the two systems. It is **not** a numerical rule for converting one mark into a virupa. Need a commented example of discord including the house, period, both testimonies and how dominance is determined.

**4. Mantresvara, Phaladipika XXIII.18-24.** [Sastri, bilingual chapter, catalogued 1950](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621595.html); [Kapoor, English translation and commentary, PDF pp229-231](https://www.panchanga.lv/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Phaladipika_Shri-Mantreswara.pdf#page=229). Both specify summing by rasi. Kapoor supplies seven charts and a worked cumulative plate. Verse 21 differs in emphasis: Sastri describes selected houses/combined dots; Kapoor describes assessing qualifying houses. Neither translates it as the seven-recipient moving diagonal. OCR is a locator, not a diplomatic transcription; do not freeze v21 until the Sanskrit, commentary and worked chart agree. Intra-sign contributor (kakshya) illustrations in the notes are separate from v20.

**5. Same Kapoor witness, XXII.18-20, PDF pp214-215:** retrograde/exaltation can treble **period years** in the named longevity method; in overlapping multipliers the worked explanation applies one strongest factor. E.g. 2 years with x2, x3, x3 -> 6, not 36. This resolves one precedence question locally, not for Trailokya or AV. **XXIV.1-3,10,16ff**, printed approx pp232-235: reduced pinda timing and topic-specific deduction of contributor marks by dignity. Example trine inputs 3,5,4; alternative reduction traditions must be kept apart. Particularly promising evidence for a conditional contributor filter, not a numerical Shadbala weight.

**6. Varahamihira, Brhat Samhita, Mars chapter VI, [Bhat Sanskrit and English edition](https://sanskrit.nic.in/books_archive/61_Brihat_Sanhita_of_M_Ramakrishna_Bhatta.pdf).** Vakra onset relative to reappearance among nakshatras defines named phenomena such as Ushnavaktra. Requires visibility and stellar position, not just the sign of d(lambda)/dt. No scalar transfer or worked currency application established. No new edition-critical verification claimed.

# Financial timing: promise, qualification, activation

In the [Santhanam BPHS witness, XLI.9 and 16-17, pp. 404-406; XLII.13, p. 415](https://storage.yandexcloud.net/j108/library/hr6fkynz/Maharishi_Parashara_-_Brihat_Parasara_Hora_Sastra_%28Vol._1%29.pdf), distinguish a natal configuration from the time its fruits are assigned. XLI.9 names Leo rising, the Sun there, and participation by both Mars and Jupiter through conjunction or aspect. With Mars present and Jupiter unevaluated this is UNKNOWN, not half a yoga. XLI.17 requires the participants' strength and disposition to qualify the reading; it gives no multiplication rule. XLI.16 assigns wealth to periods of fifth/ninth lords or associated planets. Do not hand that activation rule to every yoga indiscriminately.

**First candidate period band: native's financial adversity under XLII.13.** Let P be the planet governing the period. Require its association with at least one ruler of houses 6, 8 or 12 and the absence of any trinal lord aspect to P. If both facts and the period are established, record UNFAVORABLE for that native's finances during that period. A known trinal aspect defeats this rule, not the possibility of loss from another cause. Incomplete aspect coverage cannot establish absence. Freeze house, association, aspect, period system and year-length conventions before computing. The same natal predicate is not counted again at every bar.

## Source-defined resolution and worked counterexamples

[Brihat Jataka VIII.23 and note (a), printed p. 94](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c2/The_Brihat_jataka_%28IA_brihatjataka00varaiala%29.pdf) gives a rare explicit rule. For one planet and the same matter, one yoga on each side yields neither promised effect; the note illustrates with wealth gained versus destroyed. Two or more against one, within this stated case, favor the concordant indications. Opposed indications assigned to different planets are instead assigned to their respective periods. Store the first result as BOTH_EFFECTS_WITHHELD, not general financial neutrality. No license for counting cross-book statements, duplicate aliases, or deciding two versus two.

|Simultaneous evidence|Disposition|
|---|---|
|Favorable major period, adverse transit|Two records. No transit override without a rule for that period and topic.|
|Promised wealth, weak participant|Present configuration, qualified realization; final extent unresolved.|
|Favorable raw marks, obstructed transit|Keep mark count; suppress only the identified transit promise if its own obstruction rule is met.|
|Several aspects to the annual wealth house|Use the specific mixed provision in Hayanaratna VI.3 where applicable, not a signed aspect sum.|
|Financial harm predicate and known mitigating aspect under XLII.13|Antecedent false; no opposite prediction.|
|Opposed commodity or purchase/disposal indications|Check object and horizon first; buying now and disposing later need not conflict. If genuinely incompatible, expose both; no instruction to trade.|
|Several overlapping period judgments|Attach planet, period lineage, topic and source; merge only under a cited precedence rule.|

**Nested display.** Record mahadasha, antardasha and pratyantardasha with parent IDs and independently established start/end instants. Subdivide only under the selected school's actual calendar; the finer interval needs its own financial predicate. If absent, show the ruler and timing only. A snapshot at an exact conjunction is a marker; a repeat after retrograde motion is another occurrence, not a compounded score. A shorter adverse band can coexist within a favorable parent; neither duration nor zoom level measures force.

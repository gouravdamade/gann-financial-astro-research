# Three proposals, three levels of authority

1. **Independent planetary-strength lane.** Return (B[p], S[p,k]) in counts and virupas. Use individual qualified subcomponents before a six-part total. For fixed Raman lunar longitudes, Uccha changes 34.708353 to 30.359215 virupas between 31 May 17:00 UTC and 1 June 17:00 UTC 2025. This remains meaningful when B is unchanged. Do not interpret a decline as an adverse price change.
2. **Source-governed modifier.** A text may condition a particular house or period result on the strength and disposition of a lord. Store (predicate, domain, effect, exceptions, source) as one rule. Phaladipika XXIII.23-24 supplies counterexamples to “strong means good”: dignity and which house is ruled matter. Only apply within the stated bhava judgment; if required lordship or precedence is not available, return UNRESOLVED. Do not multiply by an inferred factor.
3. **Experimental weighting.** Given nonnegative known k_p and nonzero sum, w_p=k_p/sum k. I_c=sum(w_p B_pc), in weighted marks (0..8). Use explicitly shared k for a comparable pair; chart-specific weights give a different estimand (composition AND configuration). Negative Drik totals and undefined strengths cannot be normalized this way without a further rule; do not clip. Common rescaling leaves I invariant. Example: unmeasured hypothetical strengths [60,60,60,60,60,120,60] yield 39/8 vs 33/8 marks in the common scene: difference .75 rather than 4/7. These are test coefficients, not measured Shadbala. A zero weight hides that recipient; the output is not more certain.

A virupa is a strength unit, not a favorable mark. Raw multiplication without normalization produces mark-virupas; no classical conversion into realized benefit found. Shared sky-only weights can alter the contribution of genuinely chart-conditioned counts, but do not themselves supply currency-specific strength. Release sequence: separate component first, scoped result predicates second, weighting last as a labeled ablation only.

Nine independently additive sectors agree. Three would require supplying an unprinted value; none has been inserted. The cumulative diagram itself totals 337. This is evidence for sign-column summation and instructive about missingness, not certification of the underlying horoscope, all seven masks, or the hypothetical seven-moving-sign sum. The worked result belongs to the commentary, whereas the aggregation instruction is in verse 20.

# What financial question is this chart answering?

**Research decision.** Start with the owner of the outcome, not the sign of a planet. Personal resources, source of livelihood, annual trading income, local commodity price and an FX quotation are five different observables. Even a true statement about a merchant's income would not determine whether the goods appreciated: volume, costs and inventory matter. Neither establishes which currency appreciated relative to the other.

## Worked financial reasoning

1. **Livelihood, a source's own hypothetical example.** In [Brihat Jataka X.1 note (c), pp. 114-115, and X.3](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c2/The_Brihat_jataka_%28IA_brihatjataka00varaiala%29.pdf), begin from Cancer rising. Counting ten signs inclusively selects Aries; its ruler is Mars. The example supplies Sagittarius as Mars's ninth-part placement, selecting Jupiter. The output is the Jupiter class of livelihood (including learned occupations); the note attributes timing in Jupiter's period to Garga. This is one branch from the ascendant, not three corroborating votes: Moon and Sun origins require their own inputs. The note supplies a placement, not a longitude from which to reproduce it. No dated earnings or amount.
2. **Commercial income for a person in an annual revolution.** [Hayanaratna, Gansten, VI.3, opening judgment and subsequent quoted authorities](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500953.html): inspect the second house, its ruler, and benefic or malefic participation. The opening judgment explicitly includes gains through trade and distinguishes mixed testimony. A later rule involving the rulers of wealth and its sahama distinguishes ease from difficulty according to aspect relationship. Difficulty acquiring wealth is not the same output as loss. Do not import a Parashari aspect or strength threshold into a Tajika judgment.
3. **Inventory decision, not merchant nativity.** [Brihat Samhita XLII.1, 7, 14](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brihat-samhita/d/doc229187.html): consider, for example, a documented halo at the specified lunar phase with the Sun in Virgo. Verse 7 assigns particular goods including horses and a sixth-month disposal condition. The text's stated doubling is a conditional historical claim about value at disposal, not a measured return, certain outcome or height for an oscillator. Sun in Virgo alone does not satisfy the antecedent; no local omen record means unknown. Verse 14 also requires attention to sign strength; no tie-break for concurrent contrary testimonies is assumed. Reconstruct calendar and locality before assigning dates.
4. **Exchange rate.** None of these witnesses defines a dollar nativity, a yen balance sheet or a mapping from either one's wealth to USD per JPY or JPY per USD. Preserve hypothesis IDs for both sides; the direct classical FX query is NOT_APPLICABLE.

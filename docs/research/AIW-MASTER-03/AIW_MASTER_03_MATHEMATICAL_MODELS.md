# Seven operational alternatives

Common domain: named chart, known UTC, identified source edition, reduction stage and valid input support. Unknown input invalidates only dependent projections. No outcome data or fitted parameters. Each scalar's range is a mathematical bound, not a realizable frequency distribution. S and E are additive in table entries but discontinuous in moving sign; H is non-additive, P is set-valued, W and G are nonlinear in joint inputs, V need not admit a scalar.

## S. Single-sign cumulative lookup

S(c,z)=sum_p H(p,c,z); optional S(c,z_anchor(t)).

- Status/source: SOURCE_EXPLICIT. Aiyar IX masks; Phaladipika XXIII.20 and BPHS Samudaya 72 support column operation, not cross-edition thresholds.
- Inputs and output: Seven recipient counts at one sign; 0..56 unreduced marks per sign.
- Assumptions: Anchor choice is engineering; no weighting.
- Time/interaction: Atomic change at ingress of selected anchor; same sign, same result regardless of other transits.
- Worked case: USD Aries 43 versus JPY 31: difference 12 marks.
- Failure test: All planets occupy different signs: S does not describe all their occupied signs.
- Pair: Subtract same sign/stage/profile only.

## E. Equal-weight moving-sign mean

E(c,t)=sum_p H(p,c,z_p(t))/7.

- Status/source: ENGINEERING_TRANSFORMATION. Aiyar IX unreduced only; universal phala interpretation SOURCE_SILENT.
- Inputs and output: Seven simultaneous recipient counts at their own signs; 0..8 mean marks; pair -8..8.
- Assumptions: Interchangeable unit of counting, not realized effect; all weights 1/7.
- Time/interaction: Half-open step; evaluate all new signs before publishing a simultaneous transition.
- Worked case: 32/7 vs 28/7; difference 4/7.
- Failure test: One cell +4 offsets four -1 changes; identities and triggers disappear in sum.
- Pair: Yes for descriptive mean marks.

## P. Weight envelope and dominance

P(t)={w dot (B_U-B_J): w>=0, sum w=1}=[min d,max d].

- Status/source: ENGINEERING_TRANSFORMATION. Same inputs as E; no classical probability for weights.
- Inputs and output: Seven paired differences of raw counts; Closed interval in -8..8 marks.
- Assumptions: Same unknown nonnegative weights on both sides, unconstrained except simplex; not a confidence interval.
- Time/interaction: Piecewise extrema; ties may produce flat envelope while components change.
- Worked case: Scene [0,2]; any positive weights give a positive difference, boundary weights may tie.
- Failure test: Opposing changes yield both signs: no unique ordering.
- Pair: Paired projection only; difference of separately optimized envelopes is incorrect.

## H. Source-governed interaction field

H(c,t,topic)=evaluate applicable source predicates in their own stated order; return set of compatible verdicts.

- Status/source: SOURCE_SUPPORTED_RECONSTRUCTION. Phaladipika XXVI.6 obstruction; Sudarsana house rules; Saravali natal conjunctions remain separate profiles.
- Inputs and output: Named predicates, target, actor set, house, exceptions, phase and profile; Categorical: inapplicable, supported, obstructed, mixed, unresolved; no universal amplitude.
- Assumptions: Only a text-specified priority can resolve a conflict. Simultaneous testimony enters as a set.
- Time/interaction: Change when any premise changes; exact-time if persistence not proven.
- Worked case: Mercury 4 with nonexempt blocker at 3: favorable judgment obstructed.
- Failure test: Counting benefic aspects cannot rule out an overriding obstruction; missing blocker data cannot prove absence.
- Pair: Compare named states side by side; subtracting categories undefined.

## W. Strength-weighted marks

W(c,t)=sum_p k_p(t)*B_pc(t)/sum_p k_p(t).

- Status/source: EXPERIMENTAL_HYPOTHESIS. Named strength component only; no source bridge to AV.
- Inputs and output: Counts and a single commensurate nonnegative strength per planet; 0..8 weighted marks; pair -8..8 with common k.
- Assumptions: No negative, missing or all-zero k. Weighting is a hypothesis, not an explicit effect.
- Time/interaction: Piecewise continuous for continuous k, jumps at AV or strength-rule boundaries.
- Worked case: Synthetic 2x Venus gives 39/8 vs 33/8.
- Failure test: Large strength on a low-mark planet reduces the average; stronger is not necessarily favorable.
- Pair: Only identical weighting policy; disclose any chart-specific weight composition.

## G. Geometry-modulated count contrast

G*(c,t)=R1(t)*E(c,t).

- Status/source: EXPERIMENTAL_HYPOTHESIS. AVG10 geometry + E; attenuation has no classical authority.
- Inputs and output: Counts and dimensionless R1; 0..8 mark-equivalents; pair -8..8.
- Assumptions: Concentration, not sign-bearing phase, chosen for nonnegative scaling.
- Time/interaction: Continuous geometry where evaluated; jumps in E; no interpolation of sampled inputs.
- Worked case: Scene pair .573790*(4/7)=.327880.
- Failure test: Equal antipodal clusters erase nonzero E; attenuation is not evidence of cancellation.
- Pair: Common R1 preserves weak ordering; zero collapses strict ordering.

## V. Typed multiscale state with selective projections

V(c,t)=(B_1..B_7, S_components, motion, geometry, scoped rule states, validity); return pi_topic(V) only when licensed.

- Status/source: ENGINEERING_TRANSFORMATION. Per-coordinate evidence from Aiyar, named Bala profile, astronomy and named contextual rules.
- Inputs and output: Counts, virupas, deg/day, dimensionless moments, categories remain separate; Product space with per-coordinate domains; no universal scalar.
- Assumptions: No hidden projection or rescaling; keyed identities and evidence stages.
- Time/interaction: Union of proven transition sets, exact-only observations and evaluated continuous coordinates; simultaneous changes atomic.
- Worked case: (USD B=[3,5,4,5,5,7,3], lunar Uccha=34.708353, R1=.573790).
- Failure test: Collapsing this tuple erases domain and unit; one absent source should not blank independent coordinates.
- Pair: Only typed comparable coordinates subtract; category pairs do not.

Feasibility: S/E/P require only frozen masks and read-only replay; H is feasible rule-by-rule; W is blocked for total Shadbala; G is mechanically feasible but lacks an effect bridge; V can begin as a schema without admitting any new operator. Equal-weight E is an average, whereas the display could alternatively expose 7E (total counted entries); never change scale without naming it.

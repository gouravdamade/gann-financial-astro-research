- Ephemeris/time/frame -> transit longitudes -> signs -> H lookups -> B -> E, P. Natal longitudes + Lagna -> contributor signs/masks -> every H. Reusing a reference across seven **distinct** mask entries is legitimate for a tally, not evidence of seven independent causes.
- Same transit longitudes (plus three outer bodies) -> Z1 -> R1, phase and both currency projections; Z2 -> R2. Natal seven longitudes -> N_c -> G_c. Do not add the collective as an eleventh body. G_U,G_J and their difference are linearly dependent. 365 fixed daily instants at 17:00 UTC: Pearson correlation between raw total difference and G_U-G_J = -0.294264. Descriptive within this panel, not evidence of causal independence or predictive value.
- Longitudes + mean places/velocity -> motional classification, station, sign residence, Chesta. Dignity and position -> Sthana. Directed separation and nature -> Drik. Sun/Moon angles -> Uccha, Paksha and lunar I/K. Thus full strength plus its own components is not additional testimony. Root facts shared by two distinct rules do not alone invalidate either rule; the claim of independent votes is the error.
- Epoch, site -> houses/rulers, sunrise and periods -> some Kala/Dig and contextual judgments. Label shared-sky vs local/natal dependence. Duplicate rule IDs on different subjects are distinct only if subject is part of identity.

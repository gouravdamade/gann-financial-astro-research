# The index determines the meaning

Bhinnashtakavarga: one recipient by twelve signs. Sarva/Samudaya tally as examined here: sum the seven recipient rows down **one column** S_c(z)=sum_p H[p,c,z]. The complete twelve-sign table totals 337 under Aiyar's masks. This invariant is independent of the natal layout; each contributor contributes once per listed relative place across twelve signs. It does not imply 337 points distributed over a temporal series.

**Worked-text controls.** Aiyar, Brihat Jataka IX.1-8, 1905 pp97-101, supplies masks and qualified interpretation. In the inherited Sastri notes/plate pp211-213 the example has Aries 34; Aiyar's different p100 horoscope gives Aries 4+1+3+5+5+4+2=24 from its own inputs. Neither is a time-indexed diagonal sum. The local USD Aries example is 43 and JPY Aries 31. They are research-chart applications, not printed examples.

**Closest additional witness:** [Phaladipika XXIII.20-22, root and Sastri translation](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/phaladeepika-by-mantreswara-text-and-translation/d/doc1621595.html). Verse 20's translation explicitly sums by sign and qualifies transit *over that sign*. Verse 21 examines favorable houses reckoned from the Moon and occupancies: genuine selection across houses, but not seven simultaneous recipient-specific lookups. Equality at 28 is not settled by the >/< wording. Verse 4's Moon-from-Jupiter variant is a concrete reason **not** to certify a Sastri/Phaladipika interpretation using Aiyar masks indiscriminately. Use a single witness end-to-end for interpretive output.

The digital BPHS Samudaya chapter 72.1-6 also uses a twelve-sign distribution with different bands, not a one-size-fits-all 28 threshold. Numerical tally and its interpretation need separate witness labels. Trikona and ekadhipatya reductions and subsequent pinda procedures alter a table or its derived subject; do not substitute a reduced score into an unreduced sum or conflate printed dot conventions. A worked same-chart before/after reduction and explicit stage of interpretation would be needed to add such a lane.

**Feasible source-oriented time readout:** show S_c(z_Moon(t)) and its active sign, with all seven terms; Moon is an explicitly chosen probe, not the composite ruler of seven moving planets. Other anchors are different selectors. No worked primary witness found for sum_p H[p,c,z_p(t)] as a common realized effect. Absence here is a bounded finding, not proof none exists.

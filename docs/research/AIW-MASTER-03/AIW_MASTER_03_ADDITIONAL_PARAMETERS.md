# Parameters worth retaining, and why

|Candidate|Domain and useful input|Allowed role / missing condition|
|---|---|---|
|Dignity, D1/D9, mulatrikona, D30|Planet, sign/degree, exact division, lord and relation|Category or named score in Sthana. No generic uplift; D30 ruler need not determine a unique divisional sign.|
|Functional nature and lordship|House system, site, ascendant, houses owned, subject house|Realization condition, not a fixed signed planet weight. Phaladipika XXIII.23-24 explicitly distinguishes ownership from dignity.|
|Multiple aspects|Unique aspector, target, orientation, nature, angular rule|Signed virupas only within Drik; categorical testimony/majority only within its own house judgment.|
|Vedha|Named planet, relative lunar place, simultaneous blocker, exception|Phaladipika XXVI.6: Mercury favorable at 4 obstructed by nonexempt body at 3; Moon exception. Suppression does not imply opposite effect. Negative evidence requires complete obstruction coverage.|
|Multi-body configurations|Exact set, simultaneity, natal vs transit, house|Saravali XVIII.20-21: exchanging a participant changes a named natal configuration. Store one hyperedge; pairwise fragments are not partial points.|
|Period activation|Native, dasha/subperiod sequence, segmented time, governing lord|Gates a named result. A source-defined period is not a multiplier or exponential tail.|
|Applying, separating, repeated passes|Directed relative angular velocity, branch of aspect, topic|Tajika/annual chart has its own completion, transfer and obstruction rules. A geometric support interval establishes neither classical efficacy nor persistence.|
|Intra-sign kakshya|Transit longitude within sign and contributor order|Promising fast scale inside slow sign residency. Phaladipika XXIII.16-19 and Kapoor notes: one eighth of 30 degrees = 3°45'. Which contributor supplied the mark matters. Do not divide a total count by eight to infer an individual segment.|
|Reduced bindus and pinda|Versioned reduction sequence and topic|Kapoor Phaladipika XXIV.1-3 concerns a specific natal kinship topic; derived remainder indexes an activation, not an amplitude. Inspect worked reductions separately.|
|SBC / mundane rules|Nakshatra membership, affected target, vedha type, timing|Separate source subject; Trailokya ripening or Brhat Samhita's visibility-based Mars classification is not a USD/JPY bridge.|
|Latitude/longitude and civil time|House/angle and rise/set calculations, natal epoch, UTC/UT1 convention|Can alter house, day/night, lagna and masks. Keep geometry frame, ayanamsa, node policy, apparent positions, observer, calendar and ephemeris flags. Record uncertainty, especially at boundaries.|
|House strength|Cusp/lord/aspects, exact chart and bhava|Bhava bala is not graha Drik or seven-planet phala. Never reuse a house-strength worked rule to fill a missing planetary one.|

Smallest valuable source addition: a worked intra-sign contributor activation paired with its sign tally; it adds genuine resolution without smoothing. Next: a worked interaction with complete conflicting testimonies and explicit priority, not another unconditioned weight.

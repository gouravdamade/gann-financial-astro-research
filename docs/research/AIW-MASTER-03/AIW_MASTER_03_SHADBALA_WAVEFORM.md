# Strength at a time: specify whose chart, at what place

A natal calculation S_p(t_birth,site) is fixed. Recasting S_p(t,site) for successive times is a meaningful sequence of newly cast charts; it is NOT a source-established time evolution of the birth result. Geocentric planet-only components coincide for USD and JPY at the same t. Local houses, sunrise and hour can differ by site. Natal-sensitive aspects would be a third subject, not implicit in either calculation. No automatic bridge to realized transit phala.

All quantities below use virupas (60 per rupa). Distinguish the inherited BPHS English ch27 witness, Raman worked book and the actual comparator code. They are not interchangeable.

|Component|Calculation and example|Time/domain and limits|
|---|---|---|
|Sthana|Uccha = shortest longitude distance from deepest fall /3 (0..60); plus seven varga relationship scores, D1/D9 parity scores, angular/succedent/cadent placement and assigned decan score. Raman pp19,24-28 Sun: 90+30+60+15+printed 3=198. Exact Uccha 6553/2160 makes 198.033796, not printed 198.|Continuous folded angle plus discrete varga, house and decan boundaries. Total is not normalized to 60. 49/49 worked dignity cells under **declared** natal temporary friendship policy; per-varga policy only 33/49 or 34/49 under alternative D30. Not a universal ruling.|
|Dig|Shortest circle distance from weak point /3, weak point opposite strongest cardinal cusp. Sun/Mars tenth, Moon/Venus fourth, Jupiter/Mercury first, Saturn seventh. 90 degrees from weakest gives 30; at strongest 60.|Needs local angles/house convention. An ascendant-derived equal-house fallback is not an observed cusp. Changes continuously where angles defined; missing location is not zero.|
|Kala|Sum the profile's temporal subdivisions, not just a day/night bit. Implementation Nathonnatha: ghati=2.5*LMT hours; dark group=2*abs(30-ghati), light group=complement to 60; Mercury 60. Paksha: folded Moon-Sun angle/3 for bright strength, complement for opposite nature, Moon doubled. Tribhaga grants period lord 60 (Jupiter special); year/month/weekday/hour rulers, Ayana declination and war adjustments have separate rules. E.g. noon LMT Sun Nathonnatha 60, Saturn 0; these are not total Kala.|Both continuous and jumps; needs correct local day/night, astronomical sunrise/set, declination, ruler/calendar/war conventions. No certified global bound or full worked total for this profile established here.|
|Chesta|Five non-luminaries: provisional strict code uses circular midpoint of mean planet and true planet, separates from mean planet for inner planets or mean Sun for outer, divides folded distance by 3 (0..60). 90 degrees gives 30. Undefined circular midpoint must abstain. Linear midpoint in named PyJHora diagnostic is a different formula. Luminary values reuse Ayana/Paksha; strict total excludes duplicate luminary Chesta.|Mean-longitude generation is model-specific. Speed-bin diagnostics are not the chosen continuous rule. No ordinary geographic site dependence for these longitudes.|
|Naisargika|Fixed series 60*k/7, k=1..7 in order Saturn, Mars, Mercury, Jupiter, Venus, Moon, Sun. Sun 60; Saturn 8.571429. A constant curve is correct.|No transit clock; not evidence of varying force.|
|Drik|Signed sum of directed contributions to one target in one chart under one profile; see DRIK_AND_CONCURRENT_ASPECTS. It can be negative. Raman pp85-89 and BPHS 27.19 are not one evidence identity.|Mixed piecewise angles, conditional natures and special rules. No universally certified range for the net across profiles.|

**Reproduction limits.** Raman R1.5-R2 certifies a worked example's 49 scores under declared conventions, not the unique textual authority of natal friendship carryover. The printed Sun Drik net /4 reconciles, not all Moon, Jupiter and Saturn input contributions. Printed Moon/Mercury/Venus Saptavarga subtotal anomalies stay in the historical ledger. Don't issue a six-strength total on the strength of a passing component. Unclosed: Article 136 body, D30 sign ambiguity, Sun Uccha precision, mean-place generation, nature/normalization and full chart/epoch-specific numerical fixtures.

**Temporal contract:** specify source version, subject, site, zodiac, house definition, calendar and time scales, mean/true positions, contributors, provider, validity window and precision per component. Evaluate continuous quantities at actual query times; split discrete terms at evidenced boundaries; flag undefined regimes. Never extend an exact snapshot across an event's support without evidence. Missing required term makes total unavailable but preserves known terms. An independent lunar Uccha lane is ready for a research prototype, not a complete Shadbala oscillator.

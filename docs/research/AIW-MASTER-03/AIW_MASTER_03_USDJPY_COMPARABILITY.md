# What may be subtracted

Freeze a key containing chart identity/epoch/site, source and mask revision, reduction stage, planet and contributor ordering, subject, timestamp/time scale, coordinate convention, coverage, version and units. Side ID and natal input differ intentionally. The rest must be compatible; a known USD count is not a substitute for missing JPY. Both research charts are hypotheses, not currencies with verified astrological birth events.

|Projection|Two-side test|Interpretation|
|---|---|---|
|S|Same selected sign or explicitly named common anchor and all seven rows|Difference in raw cumulative entries for the same sign; never difference of two unrelated topics.|
|E|Same seven simultaneous UTC lookups, same profile and 1/7 weights|Difference in mean favorable entries; total version differs by factor 7.|
|P|Same admissible w on both sides|Exact set of contrasts, not independent min/max on each chart.|
|H|Same source domain/rule, full premises per side|Pair of labels. No subtraction or numerical order without an explicit scale.|
|W|Same strength source, unit, site/epoch interpretation and weight convention|Shared weights isolate mark differences. Side-specific w produces extra sum((w_U-w_J)*B_J); disclose separately or withhold.|
|G|Same verified sky timestamp and cadence; same ten bodies and coefficients technique, distinct correctly bound natal refs|Difference in geometry or attenuated marks; no auspicious meaning.|
|V|Compare coordinate by coordinate, preserve source references and validity|Mixed units forbid subtracting the whole tuple as a scalar.|

A common sky-only S(t) minus itself is exactly zero; it does not establish no currency influence. Conversely, a nonzero natal-conditioned difference identifies chart dependence, not pressure on an exchange rate. Four levels: (1) arithmetic description available now; (2) relative classical result requires a matched topic, strength/realization rule and a source-authorized comparison between the two subjects (not established); (3) hypothesized financial pressure requires a separately preregistered bridge (absent); (4) financial validation would require genuinely independent outcome evidence. Neither level 3 nor 4 was attempted.

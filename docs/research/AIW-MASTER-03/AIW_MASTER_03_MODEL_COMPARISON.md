# How the candidates trade off

**Financial-interpretation amendment.** The ranking below compares numerical/descriptive research candidates, not their ability to forecast money. For a financial *question*, prefer V (typed field) containing H (a named source rule); E/P is an adjacent numerical reference, not an answer. BJ VIII.23 can resolve certain opposed wealth judgments without assigning them weights. A naked numeric curve loses that distinction; a categorical-only display loses the original marks. See the [hybrid comparison](AIW_MASTER_03_HYBRID_INFLUENCE_ARCHITECTURE.md).

**Ranking:** for source fidelity S then domain-specific H, not W/G. For a mathematically identified research contrast E with P, then S; H is not a scalar and has no scalar rank. For practical immediate utility E/P first, S as control, V as long-term architecture, H as modular enrichment, W and G last. No arbitrary numerical score assigned to incomparable criteria.

**Sensitivity:** for d across 365 days, 91.399% of nonboundary replay time permits either sign on the full shared simplex; no intervals of strict all-vertex dominance. This is a conservative parameter family, not a claim that every small perturbation of equal weights reverses it. January scene reverses under strictly positive weights (worked example 18). Restricting weights closer to uniform would narrow the envelope but needs an independently stated reason. With any L1 deviation epsilon from uniform the change in contrast is bounded by epsilon*(max(d)-min(d))/2, since weight deviations sum to zero. In January the bound guarantees the mean's positive sign for epsilon < (3/7)/2.5 = 6/35. Do not pick epsilon to preserve a desired sign.

# Share the clock, not the units

Field A stores numerical records with unit, subject, calculation profile, provenance and coverage. Show edition-specific raw marks and any individually reconciled strength in separate lanes. The seven-term moving USD-minus-JPY mark contrast remains an explicitly experimental descriptive transform. Ten-body geometry remains geometry. No identified numerical lane is validated financial amplitude.

Field B stores the [typed predicates and judgments](AIW_MASTER_03_CATEGORICAL_BINARY_INFLUENCE.md). Natal wealth, annual commercial income and locality/commodity judgments have distinct subjects. A research currency chart may have an astronomical input but that does not turn it into a person or merchant.

|Design|What is gained|What would be lost if used alone|
|---|---|---|
|Numerical only|Reproducible seven-planet marks and contrasts|Cannot represent why a rule failed, was obstructed or had mixed testimony.|
|Categorical only|Preserves scope, conflict and timing|Hides real differences in source counts and astronomical geometry.|
|Hybrid aligned lanes|Inspect quantity, condition, timing and original inputs at the same UTC|Requires separate axes, units, chart IDs and rule attribution; no single overall up/down verdict.|

A numeric input can gate a categorical rule *when its own source defines the metric and comparison*. Preserve numeric value, units, threshold, comparator and witness as parents of the judgment. Do not also count the input as a second witness. BPHS XLI.17 is a useful **negative** fixture: its request to judge strength does not establish a Shadbala cutoff. Without the missing definition the qualification stays unresolved.

Join for display by UTC and explicit identity; never join because two dates look similar. Select a natal or annual subject explicitly. Mundane markers need their own locality and commodity, not duplicated USD and JPY votes. An inspector should answer: whose finances, which question, which passage, what is known, why now, what would invalidate this reading? Preserve original numerical output when the parallel categorical lane has no result.

**Proposed first slice, not authorized implementation:** one documented human-chart period predicate, plus fixtures for missing data and subject mismatch, beside the existing unaltered raw-mark display. No automatic BUY/SELL, magnitude, confidence, order route or grand total. No financial backtest or price join.

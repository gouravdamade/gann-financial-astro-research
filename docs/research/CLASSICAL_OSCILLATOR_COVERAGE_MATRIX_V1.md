## Decision

The smallest truthful Mode 1 baseline is **empty today**. The application has
source-profiled SBC material, but its own contract correctly says founder
approval is pending and parameter count is zero. A page-supported component may
be proposed for Mode 1 only after a founder accepts the exact profile, source
packet, dependencies, and implementation version. Until then it remains
visual-only, Mode 2, or an explicit gap.

Mode 1 is limited to source-backed inputs under one approved source profile.
Mode 2 is for auditable provisional calculations and engineering hypotheses.
Mode 3 renders timing or geometry with no aggregate directional score. Classical
raw contribution, engineering normalization, and the modern USD-minus-JPY
mapping remain separate layers.

## Present Evidence Boundaries

- Page-certified SBC evidence is partial: it supports selected Vedha direction,
  nature, and individual modifier rules only inside the named editor-supplement
  profile. It does not resolve complete topology, orientation, Abhijit, Latta,
  stacking, financial conversion, or market direction.
- JHora/PyJHora agreement is a calculator witness, never a substitute source.
  Production Shadbala total and Drik remain Mode 2 because their locked external
  reconciliation has material residuals.
- The currency-relative subtraction is a transparent modern display mapping;
  no classical text can make it a classical financial prediction rule.
- Unknown, source-missing, conflicted, and dependency-blocked values are null
  gaps. They are not converted to zero, neutral, one, or a fitted default.

The acquisition priority and variable-by-variable missing evidence are in
`SOURCE_ACQUISITION_QUEUE_V1.md`; promotion requirements are in
`MODE2_TO_MODE1_PROMOTION_GATE_V1.md`.

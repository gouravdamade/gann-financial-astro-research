# Ten calculations · from a planet's longitude to an admissible conclusion

Examples 1–3 reuse a frozen astronomical snapshot. Other positions, times and numeric weights explicitly marked synthetic are calculation fixtures. A claim about the content of a book and a claim about USD or JPY carry different research classifications.

## 1. One real transit, two distinct reference charts

**Source.** Iyer gocara v4 uses the native's natal Moon sign. Vv21 and 24 give Mercury's fourth- and twelfth-place descriptions. V50 covers the whole sign. **Facts.** At 2025-05-31 17:00:00 UTC the project's Raman sidereal geocentric snapshot has Mercury 49.62844098580377° (Taurus, index 1). The USD Moon is 304.72447764829604° (Aquarius, 10). The JPY Moon is 61.08767031037019° (Gemini, 2). USD birth UTC 1776-07-04 16:56:02 and JPY birth UTC 1889-02-10 15:00:00. File, chart hashes and response-field paths are in the ledger.

**Transform.** Inclusive sign distance h=1+((1−10) mod 12)=4 for USD; 1+((1−2) mod 12)=12 for JPY. **Result.** The fourth-place description covers increase of kin/family and gain; the twelfth includes adversities concerning enemies, health and companionship. **Assumption and stopping point.** Interpreting a currency as the native is experimental. “Family increase” has no defined FX unit. The pair of placements and personal-domain analogues is reproducible. General realized effect and pair scalar are unknown.

To test genuinely concurrent active results, use the synthetic variant Sun=40° (10° Taurus) and hold Mercury's place fixed. Both placement/timing conditions now hold. **Result.** The fixture contains favorable and adverse personal claims with unknown global resolution. Assuming equal and opposite forces would erase both the domain and timing distinction.

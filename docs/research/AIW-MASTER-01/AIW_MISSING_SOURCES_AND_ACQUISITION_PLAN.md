# Ranked acquisition plan · propositions, witnesses and acceptance tests

First obtain the pages that distinguish two rival computations of the *same* configuration. A new general description of a benefic planet has little marginal value here. The tables are joined by ID. “First” means the narrow Mercury base proposal, not a general realized side magnitude. A verified publication and a legally open digital copy are separate facts.

**Evidence to collect for each candidate.** Imprint and pagination, matching incipit, source image identifier/hash, separation of root from commentary, literal domain and units, a worked same-target case, a near miss that changes the conclusion, and the exact model branch that would change. Record “not found in inspected pages” with search scope. Avoid treating that as evidence that no such rule exists in the tradition.

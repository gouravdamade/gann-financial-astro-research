|---|---|---|---|
| Source fidelity | High for the recorded proposition | High for an explicit guarded rule | Conditional on units and projection |

**Immediate choice: A.** One Mercury placement already produces a test with different results on the two charts. Mark whether each row is a general or a domain-specific reading and expose the effective-result guard state. This gives the founder a wave one can inspect point by point.

**Medium term: B.** Start with Phaladīpikā XXVI.6. It describes the relevant planet, both paired places, the exception and the output. A positive case is present in the frozen sample. One edge suffices to explain a real obstruction. The next expansion should distinguish unproved clearance from known obstruction.

**Long term: B feeding bounded instances of C.** A graph preserves meaning; quantitative projections answer specific questions where native measures and transformations warrant them. Retain the full field if only some components can be projected. A universal scalar and continuously differentiable amplitude remain unidentified. A source-specific SBC output may eventually be another independent field with an appropriate target, not a pooled input.

**Alternative ranking changes.** If the objective is only engineering workload or event density, an unsigned activity series is simpler; it belongs to astronomy Mode 3. If the objective is the proportion of a week spent in a known categorical state, Model A with a time-occupancy projection already answers it. If the objective is which of two prices will move, none of the three has established that mapping. The preserved bridge pilot supplies no basis for selecting weights.

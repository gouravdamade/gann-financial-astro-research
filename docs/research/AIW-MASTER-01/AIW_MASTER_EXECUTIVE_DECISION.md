# Astrological Influence Wave · decision for the founder
20 November 2026 · AIW-MASTER-01 · research decision

**AIW_MASTER_SOURCE_BACKED_FIRST_WAVE_READY** applies to one output: the base placement of transiting Mercury counted from a person's natal Moon, evaluated as a categorical state through the transit sign. The text also specifies qualifications on realization. A working display can faithfully expose a known base even where the effective result is unknown. Readiness here is for an implementation proposal. The USD and JPY applications each require an additional subject analogy.

At the same actual instant Mercury is fourth from the USD chart's Moon and twelfth from the JPY chart's Moon. The book assigns the fourth favorable base effects and describes adverse personal effects in the twelfth. Neither the fourth nor twelfth is a price direction. A second planet in the same sign, the Sun, is only 1.903° from Mercury in this sample. The source's qualification concerning disappearance makes a definitive realized USD benefit particularly unwarranted. The resulting pair is a useful display even with no net number. The second directly checked rule is an interaction. Phaladīpikā XXVI.6 pairs the favorable fourth with the obstructing third and exempts the Moon. Venus is in that third place on the USD chart at the same instant. In this profile the benefit is obstructed; the Iyer profile records its own qualifiers separately. In addition, two English translations of the same Bṛhat Saṃhitā verse 52 differ on whether strong opposing aspects neutralize one another. That disagreement affects the combined result and leaves the Mercury timing finding intact.

## Twenty decisions

1. **Definition.** For a named chart, source profile, subject and outcome domain, the wave returns the applicable source propositions and their status at time t. Its transitions occur when an input or an applicability condition changes. No universal causal intensity was found in the reviewed corpus.
2. **Representation.** Store a field indexed by target and outcome, with categorical entries, magnitudes in their original units and a graph of dependencies. A favorable placement, an obstructed benefit and an unknown total can then coexist without contradiction.
3. **Polarity.** Graha-gocara v4 calls particular Moon-relative positions favorable or adverse. Verse 24 describes specific adversity for Mercury in the twelfth. Verse 53 can defeat an assigned benefit. Vyas vv162–165 distinguish class-conditioned results from positional strength within Vedha. Generic nature, angular distance and unsigned strength alone cannot decide the sign of an outcome.
4. **Subject.** Gocara concerns the native whose Moon anchors the count. Natal conjunction texts concern that native and named domains. Vedha concerns the object struck in its own grid. Mundane rules can concern a named people, commodity or season. Applying a person's rule to a currency institution requires a declared analogy.
5. **Concurrency.** Group by subject, profile, target, domain and support interval. Retain all applicable assertions. Evaluate an interaction only when its complete antecedent and consequent are known. Keep two independent houses readable even when there is no chart-wide comparison.
6. **Resolvable interactions.** Verse 53 suppresses a specified good result when its predicate is established. Verse 56 changes the Moon's character with a co-occupant. Named multi-planet birth configurations can have their own result. Which of several co-occupants dominates, how two v166 modifiers stack and how cross-domain outcomes trade off remain open.
7. **Duplicate control.** Give each fact and source conclusion a stable identity. Placement can establish dignity, which can supply a modifier to one result. Keep this chain and alternate citations attached to that result. Count a second conclusion only if the rule defines a distinct outcome or target.
8. **Strength.** Preserve measure, unit, source, object and whether the measure describes ability or result. Vyas gives the same positional strength for equal circumstances in v162 but reverses the ordering of the result for the two classes. Record the specified table lookup. It gives no weight for an unrelated transit.
9. **Continuous time.** Mercury's occupancy gives a function definable at arbitrary instants with steps at sign transitions. Separate predicates can change inside a sign. The inspected verses do not prescribe a smooth intensity curve, an exact-aspect maximum or a universal decay.
10. **Waves within waves.** Use the union of real change times and the active rule graph on each interval. A long occupancy can contain several shorter source-justified conditions. The drill-down shows the same underlying intervals at every zoom level.
11. **Multiscale without smoothing.** Draw distinct intervals and summarize their time coverage, unresolved spans and boundary counts per display bin. A one-minute event remains discoverable inside a weekly bin even if its endpoints are both between sample ticks.
12. **USD minus JPY.** Subtract only matched, identified quantities on a common difference scale, with the same eligibility, origin, units, timing, treatment of missingness and justified side analogy. Otherwise return an ordered pair, matched component comparisons or a set of possible differences.
13. **Competing architectures.** Retain A, the categorical field, as the reference. B, a conditional hypergraph, handles modifiers and higher-order effects. C, a constrained numerical projection, explores magnitude and sensitivity once its parameters have meaning. Agreement among them on geometry is no validation of a scalar.
14. **First family.** Iyer's Mercury gocara placement relative to the birth Moon. Start with vv4, 24 and 50 and display v46/v53 qualification status. An unreviewed placement stays uncovered. An unknown qualifier leaves the base visible. [Source atlas](AIW_CLASSICAL_INFLUENCE_SOURCE_ATLAS.md)
15. **Missing propositions.** The principal gaps are the operational conditions for visibility and inimical aspect in this chapter, precedence among simultaneous exceptions, a worked same-target stacking procedure, duration of other effects, and a common meaning/unit for two currency charts. Each gap blocks a different output.
16. **Acquisition.** First collate this gocara chapter against a second Sanskrit/translation witness. Then prioritize a complete same-target example in Sārāvalī and Balabhadra's named annual/monthly comparison, and the original Vyas-line Vedha worked material. Audit BPHS and Phaladīpikā pages before adopting a strength total. [Acquisition plan](AIW_MISSING_SOURCES_AND_ACQUISITION_PLAN.md)
17. **Terra High.** On separate implementation authorization, build a research-only evaluator of Mercury's Moon-relative sign and base, using a pinned source table, UTC, chart hash and ephemeris evidence. Return the guard statuses and an explanation. First acceptance fixture is the real 4/12 pair.
18. **Luna Max.** Visualize the two categorical lanes and their actual change intervals, with different marks for coverage, suppression and missing evidence. Clicking a span should reveal its input signs, count and verse. Show the same evidence when moving from a week to a minute.
19. **Experimental.** Both currency analogies, equal event votes, normalization, smooth kernels, frequency bands, cross-profile reconciliation and a mapping to appreciation/depreciation are additional hypotheses. An item's classification follows the weakest dependency in its claim.
20. **Shortest path.** Pin the inspected pages and chart fixture, approve the narrow table, test wraparound and retrograde re-entry, then draw independently evaluated base states. Add a resolved modifier only after its predicates pass separate source review. A founder can inspect this first wave while magnitude and financial testing remain open.

The historical bridge recorded a negative pilot result; this decision does not reinterpret it. The R7B qualification belongs to its isolated runtime profile. No price series was used in this study.

## Minimal data contract and reproducibility

Persist a run manifest (rule versions/hash, frame, effective provider, ephemeris files, UTC convention, date range and tolerances), chart identity/hypothesis hash, raw numeric fields and their provenance, and source records carrying edition, PDF/printed page, verse, text layer, subject and direction of lookup. A computed record references these IDs and includes target, domain, interval/boundary uncertainty, stage, applicability, base/effective status, native units if any, modifier proofs, conflict alternatives, missing dependencies and research mode. A pair record links both side records and its comparability verdict. View metadata such as bin size and timezone is separate from the rule-set hash.

Sort by canonical UTC and stable IDs; evaluate all changes for one instant before emitting a state. Rebuild from the frozen input/event log, version corrections and keep earlier results replayable. Compare semantic hashes independent of display order. Interval query indexes allow timeline exploration without recomputing the astrology. Precision fields distinguish raw floating-point positions from uncertainty in an original chart time or in a source reading.

Mode 1 applies to a source-verified proposition in its own domain. The engineering form of its display stays labelled as such. Mode 2 includes the currency analogy, inferred magnitudes and explicit experimental projections. Mode 3 holds geometry and activity. Legacy enums require explicit mapping. Every proposed surface carries executionAllowed=false, financiallyValidated=false and marketDirectionAssigned=false.

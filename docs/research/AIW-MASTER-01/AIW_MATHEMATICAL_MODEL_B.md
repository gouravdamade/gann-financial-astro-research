# Model B · A guarded temporal hypergraph

**What changes relative to A.** Model A can store simultaneous facts. B explains why a result starts, why it is qualified, and which other results might change if a fact disappears. A hyperedge accepts an entire tuple of conditions. For example, a five-planet natal conjunction has one five-member antecedent. Ten pairwise conjunctions are a different collection of propositions.

Let V contain typed atoms for chart identity, planets, positions, houses, measure values, character, targets, base outcomes and qualified outcomes. A rule r=(profile, antecedent tuple, guard, consequent, scope, source locator) may read several atoms and emit one or more labelled outcomes. Time-t support is the intersection of its input validity intervals and the intervals on which its guard is true. Record the *minimal proof set* of source rules and raw facts for each derivation. Two proofs of the same proposition do not constitute two influences.

**Evaluation.** Partition by subject and source profile. Begin with immutable astronomical observations and the applicable natal chart facts. Evaluate placement before a rule that qualifies that placement. Within a stage, use explicit dependencies and evaluate simultaneous boundary changes atomically. True fires, false does not apply, unknown suspends the dependent result. A competing alternative produces a conflict only if the propositions cannot both hold in the same domain. Keep an alternative proof if one dependency expires. A cycle with no grounded interpretation is unresolved. Monotone closure is appropriate only where the profile actually defines accumulative inference.

A useful mathematical precedent is provenance algebra for positive queries, which distinguishes jointly necessary inputs from alternative derivations. Here two independent obstructors can each prove one suppression. The identities of the two proofs matter even though their count has no influence unit. Negation, explicit exceptions and unknown completeness require additional guarded semantics beyond a positive-query algebra. A first implementation can build just the place/occupant/exception chain for XXVI.6 and expose the proof surviving at each time. A weekly view reports its duration and proof changes without replacing the graph. [Green, Karvounarakis and Tannen, *Provenance Semirings*](https://courses.cs.washington.edu/courses/cse544/11wi/lectures/tannen-semirings.pdf)

**Sourced edge.** Let G mean “the source assigns a good effect to this planet.” Let D, E, A and H mean depression sign, inimical sign, Astangata and hostile aspect as used by Iyer v53. The source supports G AND (D OR E OR A OR H) → defeat(G). Emit a suppression of G, not an adverse outcome. If two disjuncts hold they are two supporting reasons for one defeat. v46 supplies a separate realization dependency. v58's general benefic-place statement is preserved as baseline. Giving v53 its particular qualifying scope is SOURCE_IMPLIED at the graph level; no priority among unrelated suppressors follows.

**A weaker interaction.** In v56 the Moon shares the character of the planet occupying with it. A single admitted companion gives a directed dependency from companion character to a qualified Moon character. The Moon's result on a house is a further edge requiring its own text. If two companions have opposite characters, neither a majority rule nor the strongest planet wins under the inspected verse. Record two candidate derivations and flag the choice unresolved. v52 groups mixed aspect and mixed occupation cases by effect without specifying an arithmetic value. An implementation may record equivalence of named cases without asserting cancellation.

**Higher-order fixture.** Sārāvalī ch18 v20 describes a particular natal five-graha conjunction with more than one domain of consequence. The source profile requires the exact membership, native and conjunction definition. A provisional witness supports seeking this hyperedge. An implementation still needs the reviewed page and an occupancy criterion. Removing one member removes the five-body proof, even though six two-body pairs remain among the four. The source grants no residual fraction of the former result.

**Replay and tractability.** Build an inverted index from changed predicates to rules. Evaluate only declared patterns instead of enumerating all subsets of planets. On sorted change times, work scales with changed inputs and incident rules plus the derivations they invalidate. Worst-case enumeration of all proofs is exponential, so share proof subgraphs and bound display expansion, never silently truncate distinctions in the underlying state. Fingerprint profile, rule, chart, time and predicate values. Comparing these fingerprints explains a changed result. This model expresses precedence and state transitions, but it supplies neither a cross-tradition order nor a metric.

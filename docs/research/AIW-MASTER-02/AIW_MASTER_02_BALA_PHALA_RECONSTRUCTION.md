# From geometry to strength and tendency

A later English BPHS witness labels strength as ch27 and the result of strength as ch28. It distinguishes positional, directional, temporal, motional, natural and aspectual strength. Units of the component system are virūpas (60 to a rūpa). A larger positive capacity can strengthen an adverse actor. The held 1899 volume has a different chapter arrangement; treating “27” as an edition-independent locator would attach this rule to the wrong subject. [Translated chs27–28, pp38–42](https://vedic-astro.s3.amazonaws.com/books/bhrihat_parasara_hora_shastra.pdf#page=38)

**Isolated positional component, ch27.1.** Define fold(x) = min(x mod 360, 360 − (x mod 360)). Ucca strength = fold(λ − deepest fall)/3. Folded distances 0°, 90°, 93°, 180° give 0, 30, 31 and 60 virūpas. A continuous longitude input yields a continuous triangular function with a change of slope at fall and exaltation. This establishes a curve for strength. Computing a succession of fresh charts is an additional interpretation if presented as the realized outcome of one fixed nativity.

**Multiple aspects, ch27.19.** A quarter of the applicable aspect amount is added for benefic and deducted for malefic aspects. Mercury and Jupiter have a special full-aspect instruction. For three ordinary classified aspects with strengths 32 favorable, 20 adverse and 12 adverse in the same unit, the contribution is 8−5−3=0. That is a net contribution to target strength. It does not erase the underlying three aspects or determine the judgment of a house. The special addend needs independent collation to settle whether its base quarter has already been included. Ch27.26–31 gives a distinct house-strength procedure. Full seven-planet Śaḍbala reproduction needs time subdivisions, divisional charts, epicyclic conventions, complete aspect interpolation, friendship, and a source-worked total. No complete strength benchmark is claimed.

**Ray method, ch28.2–6.** For Moon let a = fold(λMoon−213°), c = fold(λMoon−λSun). The second angle is the lunar motion argument specified here. Each folded angle gives a ray count 1 + angle/30 (the residual degrees doubled in sexagesimal parts). The text first forms auspicious rays (U+C)/2 and inauspicious rays 8−(U+C)/2. It then defines tendencies I = 5[(U−1)+(C−1)] and K = 60−I. They use the same 60-part scale and have only one degree of freedom. No square-root formula is used in this witness.

Illustrative sidereal Moon 303°, Sun 153° → a=90°, c=150°, U=4, C=6, rays 5 and 3, tendencies **40 and 20**. Change Moon to 306° with Sun fixed → U=4.1, C=6.1, I=41 and K=19. This isolates one variable to test the equation; the pair of positions is not asserted to be an observed ephemeris trajectory. At joint zero I=0; simultaneous maxima give 60. Choosing I−K doubles the slope of I and shifts its origin. Ch28.11–12 relates predominance in a particular strength to its planet’s related houses and period. It supplies no general annual flow, cross-domain sum or FX polarity.

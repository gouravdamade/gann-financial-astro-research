**What the new evidence buys.** The printed Mars horoscope tests a complete table including orientation, rather than a single attractive result. The Sudarśana passage supplies a falsifiable same-target majority branch. The annual example distinguishes an actual nested duration operator from display smoothing. The practitioner table proves that two factors can be combined where a table explicitly does so, and one exceptional printed cell shows why a general formula inferred from adjacent cells is unsafe.

**Locator correction for Iyer.** CIV is the chapter number in the 1946 edition and the online chapter navigation. The 1884–85 held pages have inconsistent running heads (53 and 57). Identify Iyer by chapter subject, verse and scan page, e.g. v52–53 at printed p252/PDF486. In particular, the held v53 explicitly includes Astaṅgata; that phrase is absent from the consulted digital English rendering. The two should not be treated as byte-identical editions.

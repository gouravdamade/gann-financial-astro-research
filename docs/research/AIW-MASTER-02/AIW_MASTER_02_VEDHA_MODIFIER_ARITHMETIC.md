# Vedha arithmetic and the boundary of a combined result

**Vyas 1972, printed pp37–39, verses 161–171.** Positional strength runs full, three quarters, half, quarter in own, friend, neutral, enemy sign. The Hindi exposition of vv163–165 gives results 20,15,10,5 for a benefic and 5,10,15,20 for a malefic in that order. The affected object and Vedha identity must be specified. V166 supplies individual modifiers, retrograde ×2, exalted ×3, swift unchanged, debilitated ×½. V167 calls for consideration of the several attributes. V168 begins the sign-lord relationship table. Neither gives the composite operator. Keep original stanza, Hindi numerical exposition and modern branch logic separately identifiable.

Synthetic admissible cases: a classified benefic in a friend's sign gives 15; isolated retrograde gives 30. A malefic in a neutral sign gives 15; isolated debility gives 7.5 if its debilitation sign satisfies that relationship. For a physically compatible example, Jupiter is exalted in Cancer, ruled by its friend Moon; assuming an applicable benefic Vedha while retrograde in Cancer, base 15. Multiplication yields 90, exaltation priority 45, motion priority 30. Each fits the one-modifier rules. This three-value set illustrates underidentification, not exhaustive bounds. Determine sign relation before invoking the positional result; a real exaltation cannot also be debilitation.

**A different combined calculation exists.** Rau–Choudhary practitioner compilation, Chistabo 2013, PDF pp24–26, Table XVI: a half-strength benefic for two Panchaka items gives 1;00 and for three gives 1;30. The corresponding adverse column has 0;48 and 1;12 respectively at that row. Here semicolon denotes base sixty. Converting gives 1.0 versus 1.5, and 0.8 versus 1.2 in the table’s own scale. Inputs are a degree/strength row, target count and class. Use only the displayed cells. The accompanying prose locates peak strength at the middle and describes proportional variation. This supplies a practitioner magnitude-and-count interaction. It does not decide Vyas’s retrograde-plus-exalted case. The compilation also triples swift or exalted in its p12 treatment, directly conflicting with Vyas’s unchanged swift result.

**Battle and ripening.** The 1937 Phaladīpikā editorial supplement p318 and the practitioner battle sequence differ. Respectively counts 1–5 give fear/failure/bloodshed/death/ignominy versus fear/defeat/danger/bondage/death. A count of four discriminates the readings. These are named outcomes, not interval scores. The Vyas project review locates v343 at pp75–76: a later lunar Vedha to the same identity ripens an earlier fivefold SBC identity Vedha on the relevant day. A lunar hit to a different identity fails the predicate. This is evidence for delayed realization in that family. It gives no decay constant. Obtain a worked dual-modifier horoscope and original Rājavijaya target diagram before extending these branches.

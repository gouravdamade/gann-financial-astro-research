# A hierarchy with conserved duration

Balabhadra’s *Hāyanaratna* §7.8, citing Varṣatantra 17.46–47, takes a parent period D and the deducted degrees dᵢ from the revolution. For positive total S, child i lasts D dᵢ/S. Start with the parent ruler, then continue in the stated cyclic order of degrees, wrapping at the end. Vāmana repeats the operation for a third level. The child lengths sum to D. An implementation must identify the period method, deducted rather than raw degrees, civil/solar scale, epoch and rounding convention. Ties in ordering or missing degrees stay explicit. [Critical edition, §7.8 and note 2](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500980.html)

**Venus inside Venus, two readings.** Semicolon separates integer from sexagesimal parts. The edited example gives parent 90;42,17, total 28;16,31 and Venus share 7;7,27. Direct division and multiplication give 22.85372981 days; the printed 22;51,13,26 is 22.85373148. Gansten identifies two unit/conversion errors. His corrected inputs 89;0,25 and 6;59,27 with the same total give 22.00624585 solar days from the rounded displayed inputs. The note reports 22;0,22 = 22.00611111 solar days, or 22;19,40 civil solar days. Retain the approximately 12-second residual in day units, and the much larger difference between the readings. Never pass a civil duration as solar to the next recursion.

In a Venus major period, the text differentiates a Mercury child (learning and gain) from a Sun child (danger and loss) and a Saturn child (both gain and disputes). Therefore a parent period can contain genuinely different result descriptions. The worked arithmetic establishes the first Venus-child length. The exact dates of the other children's boundaries also require their individual degrees and order. Their labels need no artificial oscillation. [Venus subperiod results](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/hayanaratna-the-jewel-of-annual-astrology/d/doc1500986.html)

**What the Shāh Shujāʿ example supplies.** §8.3 works an Ajmer nativity using 34;33 ghaṭīs after sunrise. Śaka 1538−1529=9 determines the lookup; 4;19,43,42 + 0;48,23,0 = 5;8,6,42. Two lunar months plus 27 gives 87; subtracting 87/64 gives 85.640625, rounded to 86 in the text; remainder on division by 7 is 2. The source recovers Saturday and proceeds to a geographically corrected mean Sun. This is evidence for an actual multistep casting computation. It does not establish that the Venus example is his observed outcome. §8.8–11 adds daily revolutions, daily ruler, a distinct daily-period method and planetary states. In §8.10 the teacher’s method uses natal-to-current asterism count, lunar day and weekday modulo nine. This is an alternative daily classification, not automatic recursion of §7.8. | Model | Calculation and feasible display | What happens to fine structure |
|---|---|---|
| Exact event graph (primary for gochara) | Sort actual boundary times; update affected facts only. O(E log E) for E events. Store durations per state and observed transitions in every view. | Slow and fast intervals intersect. They need not be parent and child. |
| Period tree (primary for Tājika) | Recursively partition each parent, retaining an origin and a common unit. Lookup cost proportional to depth for bounded branching. | E.g. about 22.006 solar days nested inside 89.007. Preserve both identities at every instant. |
| Compatible additive components | Sum known components sharing a native unit. Eight mark indicators sum legally; their changes can all be driven by the same transit ingress. | Eight contributions do not imply eight distinct frequencies. |
| Non-additive gate | A long eligible state survives except while a short source-specific blocker is present. | A two-hour block in a 24-hour eligible window leaves 22 hours of eligibility. No partial-day negative force follows. |
| Experimental signal analysis | On a frozen numeric series, Haar pairs (3,5) give mean 4 and half-difference −1, invertible as 3,5. | Reconstruction retains samples; discarded detail or coarse regular sampling can lose crossings. Fourier smoothing can ring at a step. |

At any instant t the same profile and input yield the same state in M1, H1, D1 and W1. Views may show duration-weighted occupancy, min/max, first/last state, transition count and time uncertainty. Do not recompute doctrine from the bar close. If source profiles place a boundary at two times, the interval between them carries two possible states.

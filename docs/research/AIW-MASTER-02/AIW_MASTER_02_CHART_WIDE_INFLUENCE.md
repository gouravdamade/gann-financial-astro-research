# A house judgment that resolves opposing testimony

The strongest bounded synthesis found is BPHS ch74 Sudarśana Chakra, especially vv7–14 in the later English witness. It supplies a hierarchy of evidence and an explicit mixed-case decision. Construct three rings with twelve houses each from Lagna, natal Moon and natal Sun. For a specified target house retain ring identity. Apply the stated special cases, distinguish occupancy from an aspect-only case, count eligible benefics and malefics, then resolve an equal count by strength when comparable. Report mixed for equality. Consult the lord for a house without an occupant or aspect. [Ch74, pp143–144](https://vedic-astro.s3.amazonaws.com/books/bhrihat_parasara_hora_shastra.pdf#page=144)

Example: choose an empty fifth house in the specified representation with Venus and Jupiter as qualified benefic aspectors and Saturn as one qualified malefic. With these classes supplied and no special exception, the chapter gives a favorable house judgment, 2 against 1. Change to Venus and Saturn alone with equal established strength and the result becomes mixed. Remove both, and the fifth lord becomes relevant. Ascribing a fourth vote to the lord in the first case would violate the fallback. Counting one physical planet twice through two reference rings or adding all its dignity descriptors requires a rule not supplied by the text. No absolute quantity of offspring, reputation or wealth follows. This establishes an operational domain branch. Full-chart automation still needs the three-ring interpretation, graha classifications and multi-body tie strength adjudicated.

**Independent horary fifth-house check.** Praśna Mārga, Raman, Part I pp440–443, vv34–38, relates strength and character of a planet occupying the fifth to children. A weak malefic supplies adverse testimony; a strong benefic gives favorable testimony subject to the eighth-lord exception. Raman’s note extends the warning to sixth/twelfth lords. V37 distinguishes whether a planet capable of both results is strong or weak. Raman discusses Mars for Leo rising to illustrate functional role. This is a querent’s chart with a defined time. Two unrelated aspectors cannot be resolved by importing that single-planet branch. [Publisher edition](https://mlbdbook.com/products/prasna-marga-part-1-english-translation-with-original-text-in-devanagari-and-notes)

A house lord can rule two houses. Keep the first derivation attached to one subject, house and domain and the second to its own target. Varga dignity is evidence for a strength predicate only under a named calculation. It is not an additional vote for every result the planet touches. Planet friendships may change a sign classification and thus a raw result; use the relationship table of that profile. In the project, functional lordship for the currency charts remains unadmitted.

Represent a chart as a map from (profile, subject, target house, result domain, interval) to signed testimony, resolved category or native quantitative pair. Pareto comparison is possible when all homologous ordered components move in one direction. For health up and resources down, the profiles are incomparable without a valuation of health against resources. The witnesses supply no such exchange rate. A chart-wide *field* can therefore carry complete recorded testimony while abstaining on its total.

## Fifth lord in the held 1899 text

Govind Sharma’s Sanskrit witness, pūrvakhaṇḍa XIV, printed p86 (PDF115), introduces the fifth-house section immediately above v88. The verse contrasts the fifth lord placed in sixth/eighth/twelfth houses with its placement in an angle or trine in assessing children. For a synthetic Aries Lagna with fifth lord Sun in Aries, the angular case is present. Moving that Sun alone to Virgo changes the input to the sixth-house case. These are textual natal testimonies, not medical statements about fertility. The result is a directly checkable single-house placement branch; additional verses on the same page introduce further conditions. Do not run it on the project’s charts without a separately admitted functional-lordship profile.

# BPHS XLII.13 and the scope of the protective aspect

The inspected page prints **त्रिकेशैः संयुता ग्रहाः** in the first half and **स्वदशाकाले धनहानिकराः** in the second. The first half also requires absence of the trinal lord’s aspect. Santhanam’s English construes association with one lord of 6, 8 or 12 and harm to finances in the planet’s own period. The verse is followed immediately by v. 14. There is no attached comment defining an orb, a fractional cutoff or an expanded association relation. [Santhanam, *Bṛhat Parāśara Horā Śāstra* I, Ranjan, XLII.13, printed p. 415/PDF 414](https://storage.yandexcloud.net/j108/library/hr6fkynz/Maharishi_Parashara_-_Brihat_Parasara_Hora_Sastra_%28Vol._1%29.pdf#page=414)

| Question | Evidence | Disposition |
|---|---|---|
| What counts as *saṃyuta*? | The printed verse uses joining, with aspect separately named in the protective condition. In the same chapter vv. 8–9, 11 expressly distinguish association or conjunction from aspect. Verse 9 explicitly identifies dispositors when it needs them. | Distinct-planet same-sign conjunction remains the baseline operational convention. No specific commentary was found permitting opposition alone or host ownership in v. 13. Separation in degrees is not specified. |
| Which trinal lords? | Same edition XXXIV.2–7 treats Lagna as both an angle and a trine, separately noting houses 5 and 9 for wealth. XLII.7 names 5 and 9 individually for a different predicate. | Same-edition support for {1,5,9}. A generic translation of v. 13 alone cannot exclude the ascendant or settle every recension. Store the set with the profile. |
| What kind of aspect? | Direct it from trinal lord to the candidate. XXXIV.11–12 expressly specifies full aspect in another yoga. HPA XIII discusses fractional and full aspects. Neither is a verse-specific instruction on minimum strength for XLII.13. | Full graha aspects remain a declared geometric profile. Do not assert either fractional sufficiency or universal full-only authority here. |
| Can “own period” be turned into Venus’s Bhukti in Ketu? | The Sanskrit refers to the subject planets’ own daśā. No nested child is named. | No extension established for this passage. The explicit period-level rule in *Phaladīpikā* XXI.1 belongs to that text. |
| Does the R44 Ketu predicate fire? | Ketu is alone in Capricorn. Saturn is its lord, in Cancer opposite Ketu. | Required conjunction false. Return CONDITION_ABSENT and NO_JUDGMENT for this predicate. These facts hold before deciding the guard. Other legitimate personal-finance judgments retain their own records. |

# Birth input and chart identity

| Field | Source reading | Replay and decision |
|---|---|---|
| Date | Later *How to Judge* prints 29/30-9-1888. Expanded *Bhāvārtha* indexed caption says 29-9-1888. | Run both civil mornings in the proleptic Gregorian calendar. The single-date reading strengthens the 29th. No original local calendar entry, weekday, tithi or conversion is documented for this birth. |
| Time | 4:35 a.m. L.T. in *How to Judge*, L.M.T. in the expanded parallel text | Local mean solar time has independent textual support. Whether a witness recorded this clock directly or a compiler converted it remains unknown. No rectification. |
| Location | 26°30′ N, 74°45′ E | Decimal +26.5, +74.75. No town identification needed. Longitude × 4 minutes gives 4h59m east. Latitude enters the ascendant calculation. |
| UT input | 29 Sep 04:35 LMT → 28 Sep 23:36 UT. 30 Sep 04:35 LMT → 29 Sep 23:36 UT. | JD 2410909.483333333 and 2410910.483333333. UT is the modern astronomical input. Historical UTC endpoints are unknown. |
| Evidence for method | HPA IX, pp. 37–38/PDF 53–54 gives longitude correction for mean time and an example for **1912**. | Supports the operation. It cannot certify the provenance of the 1888 clock reading. |
| Date discrimination | Gemini Moon in the printed chart, Jupiter opening ruler | 29th yields 82.412215°, Punarvasu (7), matching all ten rāśi labels. 30th yields 94.780184°, Puṣya (8), Saturn. The other nine rāśi labels on this latter trial still match. |
| Historical astronomical inputs | Later plate gives only signs, plus the Jupiter balance. | The original ayanāṃśa, ephemeris, lunar interpolation, recording precision and year convention remain unidentified. |

The double date also occurs in Charts 38, 40 and 41, including a chart whose clock is explicitly G.M.T. Its presence alone cannot decode a time standard or a calendar. Raman discusses sunrise days in the introduction, but supplies no rule assigning one civil morning to this particular slash. Chart identity rests on the whole plate, the repeated interpretive details and the matching caption where available.

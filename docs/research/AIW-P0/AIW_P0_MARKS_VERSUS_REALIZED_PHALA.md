# What survives the move from marks to realized phala

**The strongest conflict fixture is already in the historical horoscope.** In Aries Mars is in its own sign, but only 3 of 8 references mark it favorable. Aiyar’s p100 example cancels three favorable against three adverse contributions, leaving 2/8 adverse. Bhaṭṭotpala’s worked example likewise gives an adverse excess in Aries and an even larger 6/8 adverse excess in Scorpio, Mars’s other own sign. Own-sign dignity therefore cannot automatically replace an adverse raw majority with a favorable outcome. Aiyar's example is an explicit calculation of the mark excess. Its text gives no second numerical line showing the final adjustment for Aries. [1905 pp99–101](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/c/c2/The_Brihat_jataka_%28IA_brihatjataka00varaiala%29.pdf#page=143) · [Bhaṭṭotpala, Sītā Rāma Jhā ed., 1934 scan](https://archive.org/details/jyotisha)

| Mars transit in printed figure | Raw evidence | What the source actually adjudicates |
|---|---|---|
| Aries | 3 favorable, 5 adverse. Own sign, second from Pisces Lagna, twelfth from Taurus Moon | One-quarter adverse in the worked count. Own sign and apacaya point in different directions. No final joint coefficient is given. |
| Capricorn | 5 favorable, 3 adverse. Eleventh from Lagna, exaltation sign | Aiyar note (f) assigns full favorable effect. The quarter is the raw excess. |
| Gemini | 3 favorable, 5 adverse. Fourth from Lagna | Note (f) assigns full adverse effect in this apacaya case. |
| Scorpio | 1 favorable, 7 adverse. Own sign, ninth from Lagna | Bhaṭṭotpala p144 gives 6/8 adverse at the counting stage. The text does not give a universal adjustment for offsetting dignity. |
| Cancer | 4 favorable, 4 adverse. Debilitation sign | The signed excess is zero. Equality is explicitly middling in the commentary. No digitized measure of post-dignity harm follows. |
| Aquarius | 3 favorable, tenth from natal Moon | Aiyar’s diagram has 5 adverse. Bhaṭṭotpala p144 has 4 adverse and 1 neutral, giving 1/8 adverse instead of 2/8. |

**Why the Aquarius difference matters.** In the 1934 Sanskrit edition the gloss on IX.3, p138, discusses the tenth place from the Moon. The worked accounting in the gloss on IX.8, pp143–144, actually withholds the eighth effective contribution. Sastri, second edition, pp199, 204–205, explicitly identifies the same exception. Hence 39 favorable marks over the Mars cycle is compatible with two *different* adverse totals, 57 under Aiyar’s binary reading and 56 with one neutral place in the commentary profile. In the latter profile keep the denominator eight for an eighth of power. The neutral reference is still present. [1956 edition](https://astrojyotish.info/literature/brihat-jataka-sastri/)

**Order that can be recovered.** Fix natal signs and recipient, evaluate each reference, keep favorable/adverse/neutral identities according to edition, cancel contrary marks and determine preponderance. IX.8 then describes the ripening of the result under qualifying placements. Its Sanskrit distinguishes a strengthened favorable result and the attainment of the unfavorable. Aiyar renders the concordant cases as full realization. His house gloss uses upacaya 3,6,10,11 from Lagna, and reports an alternative reckoning from Moon. Own, friendly and exaltation signs qualify on the favorable side. Their adverse counterparts and apacaya qualify on the other. Trinal and common-lord reductions belong to subsequent uses of the table. Applying them before reproducing p100 changes the question. [IX.8 Sanskrit](https://www.wisdomlib.org/hinduism/book/brihat-jataka-by-varahamihira-sanskrit-english/d/doc1501544.html)

**What the commentary adds.** Jhā’s 1934 print, pp145–146, distinguishes the birth configuration from the planet’s strength at delivery, cites Devakīrti and treats the Moon specially. Sastri pp205–207 describes reduction of favorable effects under adverse conditions and the need for strength at fruition. His example of Saturn third from the Moon with few marks limits the goodness of that ordinarily favorable transit. Mantreśvara XXIII.10 and its English explanatory example explicitly address three favorable marks in an otherwise favorable placement: adversity is mitigated. With five it describes enhancement. The adjustment is qualitative. The readings give a direction of modification but no agreed multiplier, timing of assessment across every witness, or way to arbitrate own sign against apacaya. [Phaladīpikā XXIII.10, printed p225](https://www.panchanga.lv/wp-content/uploads/2020/06/Phaladipika_Shri-Mantreswara.pdf)

**Decision.** Admit the raw operator with an explicit edition. Admit the two full-result examples as sourced case annotations. Withhold a general AIW_ASHTAKAVARGA_QUALIFIED_RESULT_V1. The notation “full” names a textual condition. Mapping every such description to a calibrated +1 or −1 amplitude would add an unverified measurement assumption.

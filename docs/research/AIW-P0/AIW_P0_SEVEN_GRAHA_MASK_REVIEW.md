The first four recipient rules are on pp97–98, Jupiter on p98, Venus across pp98–99 and Saturn on p99. All seven favorable masks are **accepted for this 1905 profile**. The other five have complete source lists and the per-sign counts can be calculated for any supplied reference signs. Only Mars had the twelve-cell historical worked example reproduced in this audit. AIW-MASTER-02 supplied no previous mask set for the other five. The plate scan is a distinct printed comparison. Its relationship to the manuscripts behind Aiyar is unestablished.

A further distinction affects adverse counts. The 1934 Bhaṭṭotpala commentary and V. Subrahmanya Sastri’s 1956 edition recognize an ineffective tenth place from the Moon for Mars. They still give Mars’s favorable Moon places as 3,6,11. The printed BPHS Moon-column 10 cannot be silently adopted as either the same favorable list or proof of neutrality.

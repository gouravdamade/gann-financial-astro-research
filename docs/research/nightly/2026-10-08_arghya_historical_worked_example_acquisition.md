## Direct Historical Findings And Their Limits

The printed sequence is internally coherent as a 61-67 run and contains two
related dated displays. It visibly supplies:

- a Bombay silver-forward context and stated standard-time snapshots;
- named candidate/strength-table columns;
- selected planetary/condition inputs;
- at least one narrative admission condition: only a striking planet that
  aspects the relevant Vedhya Rashi produces the stated Vedha effect;
- selected contributor classifications and residual/total displays.

It does **not** visibly supply, as a complete and independently auditable
chain:

1. the exact candidate set and full rule used to select the country, time, and
   commodity rulers;
2. the composition function for Kshetra, Vakra, Udaya, Uccha, and any other
   displayed component;
3. a rule for ties or multiple candidate rulers;
4. source-bound astronomical conventions for the values used in each component;
5. full Vedha traversal/admission and multiple-hit aggregation/cancellation;
6. every intermediate arithmetic step from the selected lords to the displayed
   residual; or
7. a page-visible, source-certified conversion from the residual or
   twenty-part historical commodity basis to a predicted price.

The price figures and handwritten decimal annotations are recorded as visible
historical content, not treated as a forecast validation, a regression target,
or a license to fit or infer a formula.

   retained; no alternate authenticated access or scraping was attempted.

   not establish the claimed Arghakand provenance.

   and repositories. They are leads only and affirm that title alone is
   ambiguous.

No lawful public discovery result yielded the underlying 1934-1952 serial or a
page-controlled source that carries the full 1951 arithmetic.

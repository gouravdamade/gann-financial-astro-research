## J. Multiple Actors, Residual, And Commodity Basis

Verse 375 source-closes only an Arghya-local residual after separately obtained
auspicious and adverse Viswa quantities:

```text
residual = auspicious_viswa - adverse_viswa
```

Verse 376 instructs that the current commodity `bhava` be imagined in twenty
parts, then that the previously obtained favourable Viswa be added and adverse
Viswa subtracted. As a scoped historical commodity-basis statement, this can
be expressed without adding a price equation:

```text
commodity_basis_parts = 20 + residual
```

The text connects an increase in the commodity basis with abundance and price
decrease, and a decrease with scarcity and price increase. It does not equate
one Viswa to a modern percentage return, provide a modern price equation, or
map the result to an FX pair. `Bhava`, `mulya`, availability, quantity, and
price remain distinct source concepts.

The netting instruction does not itself establish how multiple actors are
selected, whether every candidate Vedha enters a total, or what happens where
several strengths/aspects compete. No primary worked case located in this pass
supplies that missing aggregation or precedence.

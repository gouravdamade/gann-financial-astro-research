### BPHS root rule / Padmanabh witness

BPHS 3.11 gives:

`tatrārka-śani-bhūputrāḥ kṣīṇendu-rāhu-ketavaḥ / krūrāḥ śeṣagrahāḥ saumyāḥ krūraḥ krūrayuto budhaḥ`

Padmanabh Sharma's Hindi commentary explicitly explains that Sun, Saturn, Mars, waning Moon, Rahu and Ketu are cruel planets; the remaining planets are benefic, and Mercury becomes cruel when **joined with a cruel planet**.

This closes only the following narrow statements for the BPHS/Padmanabh nature profile:

1. **Conjunction/association is stated.** The operative word is `yuta`; this verse does not say that a mere aspect to Mercury changes Mercury's natural class.
2. **No proximity/orb threshold is supplied.** The verse does not say "within N degrees" or establish a nearest-planet tie-break.
3. **Sun is already in the cruel list.** A Mercury-Sun conjunction is therefore covered by the plain `krūra-yuta` wording. The verse does **not** independently state combustion as a separate Mercury-nature switch.
4. **Rahu and Ketu are explicitly in the cruel list.** On the literal construction, conjunction with Rahu or Ketu satisfies the same `krūra-yuta` condition. This is a source-text implication, not a separate modern-node convention imported into BPHS.
5. **Mixed benefic + malefic association remains not explicitly adjudicated.** The verse gives a sufficient-looking cruel-association condition but provides no worked multi-association example or cancellation/precedence rule. Do not code "closest wins", "benefic overrides", "malefic always overrides", or an aspect-vs-conjunction hierarchy beyond the fact that `yuta` itself is what this verse states.

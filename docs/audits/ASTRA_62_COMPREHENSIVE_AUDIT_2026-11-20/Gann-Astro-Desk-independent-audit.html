05 — Mathematical foundations

Let T = mean(exp(iθₖ)) over the transit bodies and N = mean(exp(iφⱼ)) over the natal bodies. Define P as the mean of cos(θₖ − φⱼ) over all ordered transit/natal pairs. Expanding exp(iθₖ)exp(−iφⱼ) and taking the real part gives P = Re(T conjugate(N)) = TₓNₓ + TᵧNᵧ. This proves the identity for 49 and 70 terms without using application code. Both means divide by their membership count. Normalizing T or N to length one would silently remove coherence and change the signal.

For fixed natal vectors U and J, D = Pᵤ − Pⱼ = T · (U − J). Consequently |D| ≤ |T| |U − J| ≤ |U − J| ≤ 2. The actual bound for the declared references is 0.8439451117. Positive means the transit mean projects more strongly onto the USD reference. Zero occurs at T = 0 or when T is perpendicular to the reference difference. Swapping pair order reverses the sign. Common rotation of all three vectors leaves P and D invariant. An epoch-dependent rotation or change applied to transit alone need not cancel.

Their determinant is −0.24470523. Given both projections and these two known axes, a 2×2 linear solve reconstructs T. Given D alone, the component perpendicular to U−J is lost. Even perfect knowledge of T cannot identify the planets behind it. As a counterexample, five bodies at 0° plus five at 180° and five evenly spaced antipodal pairs can both have T = 0. The former has |mean(exp(2iθ))| = 1, while a regular decagon has 0. Thus R2 contains genuine complementary information. Circular variance 1−R1 is a re-expression of R1. A valid zero vector has a defined zero projection and an undefined heading. A missing body invalidates the specified population.

The oracle checked 1,024 seeded skies and both member counts, including rotation, permutation and known antipodal examples. Worst direct/factorized difference was 1.95 × 10⁻¹⁶ or less. At 2025-05-31 17:00 UTC the direct 70-term result was USD 0.2213210042, JPY 0.1247132838, difference 0.0966077204. The Swiss-file working service agreed within floating-point rounding. A +1 h shift to the USD or JPY natal time changes its mean vector by 0.001358 or 0.001239 respectively. These are upper bounds on the corresponding projection changes for any transit vector, a local sensitivity experiment with no market selection.

09 — Multi Oscillator design

The seven-body regression experiment and ten-body display have separate identifiers and different transit inputs. The first harmonic T is efficient and sufficient for every equal-cosine currency projection in the brief. It discards body labels, individual motions, pairwise configurations and higher harmonics. A second moment R2 recovers some bipolar structure. It cannot restore all ten longitudes. The current response already includes each longitude. Retain these as the canonical observations.

Proposed quantity What it contributes Decision
Per-planet signed term Re(exp(iθₖ) conjugate(U−J))/10 Attributes D to a body. The ten terms sum exactly to D. Expose on inspection. Never count D again as an eleventh contribution.
Relative separation and speed A specific pair can approach an aspect while the mean vector is unchanged. Signed speed identifies turning behavior. Add as named diagnostics with body IDs and UTC. Derive motion on a continuous arc or from ephemeris speed.
Second or higher harmonics Distinguish selected angular symmetries that share the first moment. R2 already has a defensible purpose. Add a higher order only for a named question.
Weighted or fast/slow means Change which bodies contribute; groups can expose cancellation. Declare weights and overlap. A fitted weight must carry a training population and mode.
Circular cross-correlation First-harmonic correlation is another projection. A full angular distribution retains higher-order alignment. Specify which orders or labelled pairs add information.
Frequency spectrum or wavelets Describe temporal variation or localized changes across a sufficiently long sampled record. No independent planetary input appears from transforming one plotted series. Freeze window, detrending and sampling rules.
Finite differences and acceleration Add temporal context absent in an instantaneous longitude. Amplify noise, irregular sampling and wrapping errors. Compare against returned speed and controlled dense sampling around stations before display.

For a retained angular feature of harmonic k moving at maximum rate ω degrees/day, enforce k·ω·Δt < 180° to disambiguate successive wrapped samples. A practical accuracy target can be much tighter, e.g. k·ω·Δt ≤ 2° when resolving two-degree changes. Estimate ω from the chosen body and frame. M30/H1 suit inspecting intraday events, H4 suppresses shorter structure, daily may suffice for slow-body trends. A 2° display tolerance is a proposed specification. No single cadence resolves every threshold crossing. Bracket sign changes, use a root solver for exact events, preserve missing spans, and never use render-decimated points for numerical decisions. Unwrap only through valid consecutive samples. Near zero coherence the mean direction is ill-conditioned even when every longitude is known.

For a Kolkata overlay, global geocentric ecliptic longitude does not depend on Kolkata. Topocentric altitude, azimuth, rise/set, houses and the local vertical do depend on location, time and potentially refraction. Newtonian acceleration scales as GM/r², potential as −GM/r, and leading differential acceleration across a baseline as GM·L/r³ with angular dependence. A terrestrial tide is a differential field. A gravitational wave is propagating spacetime strain. No measured strain series, calibrated detector, source orientation or causal financial mechanism was supplied. A physical overlay would need positions/distances, units, observer coordinates and an observable defined in advance. The equal-weight cosine is dimensionless and contains no mass or distance. 16 — Architecture simplification

A single immutable sample can support the chart, timeline, decomposition and export. Define it by timestamp/time scale, requested and effective coordinate profile, versioned provider/files, ordered body set with labelled longitudes, validity and reasons. Derive separate views for the collective moments, the natal projections and an individual body’s contribution. The chart selects observation times from market candles. A standalone timeline selects its own observations. Neither sampler should define the astronomy formula.

The current phase builder computes a collective field, then forms another transit vector from the same longitudes for the projection. It calls the shared numeric helper, but the data traversal and missing-value logic are duplicated. A sample exposing the unrounded Cartesian components would eliminate that second pass without reconstructing from rounded R1 and heading. Use labels rather than positional ordering at API boundaries. Keep the uncertainty caused by a missing body distinct from an exactly cancelling vector.

The common engine, SBC and timing paths have different provider and fallback contracts. Alias of a lock cannot guarantee an entire configuration-plus-calculation transaction is owned by that lock. In particular financial_astro_ephemeris._calc_longitude originally discarded the provider result. In the closing snapshot it appends effective flags and file identity to a per-body list, while the route still permits fallback. The permitted path can retry broadly. A long grid now repeats provenance for each body/time pair. Deduplicate invariant metadata using a profile ID only when the effective execution evidence is identical. The basic configuration loop can return a fallback string when no candidate exists without explicitly resetting a previously selected path. Test this path in a long-lived process. SBC’s existing richer provenance is a candidate for reuse after parity testing. A cache key cannot certify a response whose effective provider is unknown; store realized identity with the result and reject hits when the requested strictness or file identity differs.

Load and pin the doctrine configuration once per request, including its file hash. Pass that same configuration to the calculator and provenance builder. Repeated loads cost measurable time and leave a window in which a configuration edit could separate a receipt from the settings actually used. The timing cost was observed. A mismatched receipt during a concurrent edit remains a hypothesis. The isolated twelve-sample comparison in report 11 provides a regression fixture.

The call-site inventory records 1,076 text matches for time conversion and ephemeris operations in the feature checkout. It distinguishes test files from candidate runtime or research paths. A textual hit establishes neither reachability nor lock ownership. XE1’s checked ablation document explicitly marks direct features, derived children, modifiers, gates and unknowns as synthetic roles. Reuse that role distinction for explanations without importing its demonstration multipliers into a classical operator.

Treat mathematical semantics as independently versioned profiles. Source-defined operators can return categories, strengths in named units and unresolved states. Fitted models carry features, coefficients and population hashes. Experimental geometry carries coordinate frame and normalization. A label of “Mode 1” in the overall workspace does not establish that every visible experimental overlay has a classical source. Keep the overlay’s own provenance visible. Archived evaluators, fixed inputs and source conflicting readings retain their original identities; architectural simplification should apply to the live path.

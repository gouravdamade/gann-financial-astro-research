# Agarwal Hardcopy Gap Closure

Audit date: 2026-08-13

## Scope

This note records founder-supplied photographs of a physical Sagar Publications
copy of M. K. Agarwal's `Mystics of Sarvato Bhadra Chakra and Astrological
Predictions (With norms for financial gain in share market)` and reconciles them
against the incomplete 191-page Internet/image scan audited on 2026-07-22.

The purpose is provenance and page-gap closure. It does not by itself promote
Agarwal into classical doctrine, deterministic scoring, market direction,
Auto Suggest, live inference, validation, or execution.

## Physical-edition identity

The photographed front matter visibly establishes:

- author: M. K. Agarwal;
- title: `Mystics of Sarvato Bhadra Chakra and Astrological Predictions`;
- subtitle: `(With norms for financial gain in share market)`;
- publisher: Sagar Publications, New Delhi;
- edition statement: `First Edition 2000`;
- copyright: M. K. Agarwal;
- publication/printing statement: `Published and Printed by: Saurabh Sagar for
  Sagar Publications New Delhi-110001`;
- printer: `The Artwaves, New Delhi-110019`.

No ISBN is visible in the supplied front-matter capture. Edition identity is
therefore based on the explicit publisher and edition statements, not an
inferred ISBN.

## High-value pp.145-146 author figure

The supplied hardcopy includes one photograph spanning printed pp.145-146. It
shows the complete author-published `Sarvato Bhadra Chakra` figure across the
two-page spread, including cardinal orientation, signs, navamsas, padas,
nakshatras, weekday cells, vowels/consonants and numbered vargas/cells.

This is more valuable than a simple gap repair because it is an
edition-authenticated author figure rather than a ChiStaBo redraw. It may be
used as a future page-level geometry comparison witness after a dedicated
cell-by-cell transcription/review. The current photograph is readable but the
centre fold should be treated cautiously for exact middle-cell geometry; a
flatter archival photograph may still be useful before exact transcription.

## Reconciliation with the old scan

The old `AGARWAL_MYSTICS_INCOMPLETE_SCAN_5644DFC4.pdf` remains an incomplete
file as a standalone artifact. The new physical evidence changes the acquisition
state as follows:

1. The missing eight printed pages are no longer unknown; they are repaired by
   independent photographs from the founder-held physical Sagar copy.
2. The physical front matter establishes Sagar Publications and `First Edition
   2000`, closing the prior edition/imprint uncertainty.
3. Extra neighboring page photographs and the founder's direct p.48 comparison
   support continuity between the old scan and the physical edition.
4. The two ChiStaBo files remain derivatives and are not upgraded to independent
   witnesses.
5. The combined scan-plus-hardcopy evidence is now adequate for page-level
   research across the previously missing locations, but it does not
   automatically certify every Agarwal rule as executable doctrine.

## Financial Chapter 20 boundary

Printed pp.180-194 were already intact in the old scan. The hardcopy acquisition
now supplies an edition-identified physical witness for the book, but Chapter 20
continues to be treated as modern-practitioner hypothesis material. Its
bullish/bearish, commodity, share-market, Teji/Mandi and timing claims may
nominate explicit research hypotheses; they do not become classical SBC doctrine
or live market rules by virtue of the edition identification.

## Decision

- Mark the 2026-07-22 missing-page acquisition gap as **closed**.
- Mark the Sagar Publications `First Edition 2000` identity as **physically
  evidenced**.
- Preserve the old Internet scan's SHA-256 and incomplete-file status; do not
  rewrite history by calling that file complete.
- Use the hardcopy photographs as the controlling evidence for printed pp.46-47,
  54-55, 62-63, 133 and 144.
- Treat pp.145-146 as a high-priority author-figure geometry witness for a future
  bounded transcription/audit.
- Do not enable an executable Agarwal scoring/profile layer merely because the
  acquisition gap is closed. Any executable rule still requires its own
  page-level source contract and project approval.

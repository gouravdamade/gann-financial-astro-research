## C. Graha Latta

Original scan pp.74-77 / printed pp.58-61, verses 261-275, source-close a
separate `LATTA` event type over the 27 stars Ashvini through Revati, excluding
Abhijit. Its offsets are forward for Sun 12, Mars 3, Jupiter 6 and Saturn 8;
backward for Mercury 7, Venus 5, Rahu 9, Ketu 9 and Full Moon 22.

The source passage identifies Full Moon but does not establish diminished-Moon
Latta. It also does not settle the ordinal counting origin. Both remain fail
closed. The recorded descriptive cases, the Sun-Latta occupant table and the
Upagraha+Latta+krura compound are source descriptions, not trade outcomes.

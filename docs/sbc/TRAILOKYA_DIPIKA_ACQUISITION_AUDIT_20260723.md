## Page Alignment And OCR Quality

The OCR companion contains 131,071 extractable characters, of which 99,610
are Devanagari, across 113 pages. Pages 3, 4, 14, 115, and 116 have no useful
text layer; visual inspection shows blank/front-matter pages or diagrammatic
content rather than a missing sequence.

All 118 corresponding rendered pages have matching dimensions and order.
Representative pages 1, 5, 10, 20, 40, 60, 80, 100, and 118 were compared
visually. A full-page perceptual comparison averaged approximately 0.957, with
the lower values explained by compression and contrast differences. The OCR
file is therefore a derivative of the same page sequence, not an independent
textual witness.

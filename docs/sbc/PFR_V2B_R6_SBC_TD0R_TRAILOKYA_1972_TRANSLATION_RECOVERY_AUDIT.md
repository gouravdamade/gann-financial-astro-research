# PFR-V2B-R6-SBC-TD0R - Trailokya Dipika 1972 Translation Recovery Audit

## Scope and Result

This is a source-recovery and attribution audit. It does not alter the
Trailokya runtime profile, create a new product surface, promote a source
variable, calculate a score, or enable any financial behavior.

The controlling witness remains the checksum-identified 118-page 1972 image
scan. The matching OCR PDF was used for navigation and draft translation only.
The 2016 Khemraj reprint was used only as a same-lineage reading witness for
the previously reconciled Arghya tables. Neither is an independent doctrinal
witness.

## Recovered Historic Artifacts

All five requested artifacts exist and their pre-audit bytes were recorded in
`configs/sbc/trailokya/trailokya_1972_translation_coverage_ledger_v1.yaml`.
The legacy page-certification packet has six entries. The two Arghya CSVs each
contain 108 rows; their reconciliation reports zero cross-edition mismatches.

## Original-Image Findings

Visual inspection of the 1972 source images confirms these narrow statements:

- scan 20, printed 4, verses 12-14: the five variable-motion bodies have
  qualitative right, left, and front directions for retrograde, swift, and
  mean movement respectively;
- scan 21, printed 5, verses 15-17: Sun, Moon, Rahu and Ketu have all three
  directions, side Vedha reaches aligned items, and front Vedha reaches the
  opposite outer nakshatra;
- scans 9-11: the practical introduction describes manual identity placement
  across name initial, nakshatra, rashi, vowel, and tithi before current planet
  placement is inspected;
- scans 29-30, printed 13-14: the source gives natural-class text and
  condition wording for Moon and Mercury, but TD0R does not convert that into a
  timestamp-safe runtime contract;
- scan 55, printed 39, verse 166: isolated retrograde, exaltation,
  debilitation and swift result wording is visible. It does not supply a
  combined modifier precedence or a financial magnitude scale;
- scans 98, 99, 101 and 102, printed 52, 53, 55 and 56: the Arghya tables and
  twenty-part availability prose are present. They do not supply a complete,
  reproducible score-to-price equation.

## Arghya Result

The two source-preserved anomalies remain exactly as printed: `11|45` and
`2|18`. The narrow arithmetic ledger may describe an availability index as
`20 + benefic Viswa - malefic Viswa`. It remains a research-only source
interpretation; it is not a price formula, a bullish/bearish label, or an FX,
stock, ML, Auto Suggest, live-inference, or execution input.

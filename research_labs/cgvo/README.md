# CGVO Research Lab

`PFR-V2B-CGVO-P1` is a read-only Classical Geography & Visibility Observatory.
The backend computes factual modern Swiss Ephemeris eclipse circumstances and
loads source-separated Varahamihira, Trailokya, and raw Kurma research
fixtures. It does not infer a market direction or combine source profiles.

The authoritative machine fixtures are under
`configs/research/cgvo/`. Unknown mappings and source-silent material remain
explicitly unavailable.

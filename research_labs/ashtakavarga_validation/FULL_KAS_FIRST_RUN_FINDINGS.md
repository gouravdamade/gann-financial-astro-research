## Market result

No tested KAS feature passed the complete evidence standard.

The smallest adjusted p-value was `0.2586`, so no comparison survived the 864-test Bonferroni family at 5%. That result was the learned-direction House 2 one-day Antardasha disposition, but its hit rate was only 45.46%, all five fold returns were negative, and its circular-shift placebo p-value was `0.98`.

The strongest fixed-direction full-method candidate was House 2, first Antardasha sector, Sun-gated, one-day:

- 904 non-overlapping observations;
- 55.42% hit rate;
- raw p-value `0.00112`;
- Bonferroni p-value `0.9645`;
- circular-shift placebo p-value `0.13`;
- median fold return `+0.0383%` before costs;
- median fold return `-0.0117%` at five basis points.

The simple House 1 one-day Antardasha score produced 53.86% over 1,359 observations, but its adjusted p-value was 1.0, circular-shift p-value was 0.935, and the five-basis-point median became negative.

Several five- and twenty-day cells looked stronger, but sample sizes were much smaller and none survived correction or placebo checks consistently. They are exploratory leads, not evidence of a reliable edge.

## Decision

- Retain the full KAS engine as an isolated research instrument.
- Do not promote any KAS feature to USDJPY, BTC, ML notes, Auto Suggest or MT5.
- Complete two outside BAV/SAV calculator checks.
- Obtain an exact improved-Krushna-ayanamsa implementation or trustworthy export before source-reproduction claims.
- Freeze an economically defensible market house mapping before an untouched holdout test. Do not choose House 1 or House 2 merely because this run looked better.
- Treat the first run as evidence against a large, obvious standalone KAS edge, not proof that every narrower hypothesis is false.
